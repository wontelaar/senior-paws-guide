## Which Raincoat Fits Your Dog's Shape and Walking Routine?

Raincoats are sized by body measurements and differ in how much of the dog they cover, so it helps to match both. It takes about two minutes.

**Step 1: Measure back length.** With your dog standing, measure along the spine from the base of the neck (where the collar sits) to the base of the tail.

**Step 2: Measure chest girth.** Wrap a soft tape around the widest part of the ribcage, just behind the front legs, snug enough to stay in place but loose enough to slide a finger under.

**Step 3: Measure neck girth.** Wrap the tape around the neck where a collar would sit, which matters for the hooded and collar-style coats.

Size charts often mix inches and centimeters, so here is a quick converter:

| Inches | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 | 28 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Centimeters | 30.5 | 40.6 | 50.8 | 61.0 | 71.1 |
