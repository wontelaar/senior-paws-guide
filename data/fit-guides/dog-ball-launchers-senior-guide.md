## Which Launcher Handle and Ball Size Fits You?

Launchers differ in handle length, included balls and whether they are hand-held or automatic, so a quick comparison points you to the right one.

**Step 1: Choose hand or automatic.** Three of these are Chuckit! hand launchers, and the MEKAPLE is an automatic launcher.

**Step 2: Compare handle length and size.** The Classic launchers have a 26 inch handle and the Sport has a 12 inch handle. The table below lists each size in inches and centimeters.

**Step 3: Check what is included.** Compare the included balls and, for the MEKAPLE, the batteries and mesh bag.

**Listed size and weight**

|---|---|---|---|
| Chuckit! Classic 26 inch with 1 medium ball | 26 x 2.8 x 2.6 | 66.0 x 7.1 x 6.6 cm | Check the listing |
| Chuckit! 12M Sport 12 inch | 12 x 3 x 3 | 30.5 x 7.6 x 7.6 cm | 5.6 oz (159 g) |
| Chuckit! Classic 26 inch with 3 balls | 29.25 x 3 x 6.38 (package) | 74.3 x 7.6 x 16.2 cm | 1.5 lb (0.68 kg) |
| MEKAPLE Automatic Launcher | 10.7 x 10.2 x 8.4 | 27.2 x 25.9 x 21.3 cm | 3.8 lb (1.72 kg) |

**What is included**

| Launcher | Included balls | Other listed details |
|---|---|---|
| Chuckit! Classic 26 inch | 1 medium ball | Hand launcher |
| Chuckit! 12M Sport | Check the listing | Recommended for kids and shorter hands, listed for medium dogs of 20 to 60 lbs |
| Chuckit! Classic 26 inch with 3 balls | 1 tennis ball and 2 Ultra Balls | Hand launcher |
| MEKAPLE Automatic Launcher | 15 mini tennis balls (2 in) | Mesh bag, 10, 20 and 30 ft distances, 3 batteries included |

**How to read it:** if you want a classic hand launcher, the two 26 inch Chuckit! Classic sets differ in the balls they include. The 12M Sport has the shorter 12 inch handle that the listing recommends for kids and shorter hands. The MEKAPLE is the automatic option and lists three launch distances and 15 mini tennis balls. Confirm exact sizes and ball compatibility on the product page before ordering.
