## Which Way Up Fits Your Car?

Measure first, then compare with the listed numbers.

**Step 1: Measure the height.** Measure from the ground to the trunk floor or the seat your dog uses, in inches.

**Step 2: Compare ramps and steps.**

| Ramp or steps | Listed length or height | In centimeters | Listed weight rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| PetSafe Happy Ride Folding Ramp | 62 in long | 157 cm | Check the listing |
| PetThem Car Dog Ramp | 71 in long | 180 cm | 250 lb (113 kg) |
| Pawccoli Foldable Ramp and Stairs | 76.4 in long, 17 in wide | 194 x 43 cm | Check the listing |
| Uross Telescoping Stairs | Trunk heights 23 to 26 in | 58 to 66 cm | 200 lb (91 kg) |
| PetRuna Extra Wide Stairs | 4 steps, 20 in wide | 51 cm wide | 250 lb (113 kg) |

**Step 3: Add a lift or a seat extender.**

| Item | Listed size | Where it helps |
|---|---|---|
| LOOBANI Hind Leg Sling | Check the listing size chart | Hind legs |
| OneTigris Full Body Lift Harness | Sizes S and up | Front and back |
| URPOWER Back Seat Extender | 51 x 23.6 in (130 x 60 cm), up to 400 lb | Back seat |

**How to read it:** if your trunk floor is between 23 and 26 inches high, the Uross stairs list that range. For a longer slope, the PetThem and Pawccoli ramps are 71 and 76.4 inches. Confirm exact dimensions on each listing before ordering.
