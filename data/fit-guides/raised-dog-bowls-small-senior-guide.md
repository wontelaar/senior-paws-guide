## How Tall Should the Bowl Stand Be for Your Dog?

Raised bowls come in fixed and adjustable heights, so one measurement and a quick comparison point you to the right stand.

**Step 1: Measure your dog's shoulder height.** With your dog standing normally on a flat floor, measure straight up from the floor to the top of the shoulder blades, in inches.

**Step 2: Decide the height you want to try.** Adjustable stands let you start low and change the height later; fixed stands keep one height.

**Step 3: Compare with the listed heights.** Use the tables below, which show each stand's listed heights in inches and centimeters.

**How to read it:** pick the height you'd like to try, find it in the second table, and use the first to choose the bowl setup you like. If you want room to change the height later, the Vantic's 6 adjustable positions (flat and 15° tilted) or the Hubulk's 3 settings let you move between low and higher heights with the same stand. If you already know the height you want, the 3-Inch Stand and the JoyoPaw Bamboo Stand give you one fixed height with no adjustment. Confirm exact dimensions on the product page before ordering.
