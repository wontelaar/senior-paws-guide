## Which Recovery Suit Size Fits Your Dog?

Suits are sized by body measurements, and each listing uses a slightly different method. Taking all of them once means you can shop any of the four with confidence.

**Step 1: Measure back length.** With your dog standing, measure from the base of the neck (where a collar sits) along the spine to the base of the tail.

**Step 2: Measure chest girth.** Wrap a soft tape measure around the widest part of the ribcage, just behind the front legs. Keep it snug enough to stay in place but loose enough to slide a finger under.

**Step 3: Measure the waist and note the weight.** Wrap the tape around the belly just in front of the hind legs, and write down your dog's current weight. Record every number in inches and centimeters (1 in = 2.54 cm, 1 lb = 0.4536 kg).
