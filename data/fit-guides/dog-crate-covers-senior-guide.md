## Which Crate Does Your Setup Need?

Crate covers are sized to the wire or metal crate they slip over, so the quickest path to the right cover is matching your crate's footprint and height to the cover's listed dimensions. Here's a two-minute way to check.

**Step 1: Measure your crate.** With a tape measure, record your crate's length, width, and height in inches — length and width along the floor, height from the floor to the top of the wire frame.

**Step 2: Note your crate's material and door layout.** Covers are listed for wire or metal crates and some leave specific openings for doors, so check whether your crate has a front door only or front-and-side doors.

**Step 3: Think about airflow.** If your dog's space can run warm, or you simply want to see in easily, a cover with adjustable openings, mesh, or roller shades gives you more control than a fully closed panel.
