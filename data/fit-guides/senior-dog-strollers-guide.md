## Which Stroller Fits My Senior Dog? Weight, Size and Entry Checklist

The right stroller matches your dog's weight, body size and how easily they can step in. Here is a two-minute way to find your match.

**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Step on a bathroom scale holding your dog, then step on alone and subtract. Or ask your vet's office to weigh them. Choose a stroller whose listed weight limit sits comfortably above this number.

**Step 2: Measure your dog's length and height.** While your dog stands, measure from the chest to the base of the tail, and from the floor to the top of the shoulders (the withers). Your dog should be able to sit, lie down and turn around inside the cabin, so compare these numbers with the interior dimensions on the product page.

**Step 3: Check the entry height.** Kneel at the doorway and measure from the ground to the floor of the stroller opening. That is the step your dog will take each time.
