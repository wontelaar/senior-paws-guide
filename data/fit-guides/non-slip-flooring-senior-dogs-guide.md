## Which Traction Option Fits My Dog's Paws and My Floors?

The best traction option depends on how your dog feels about wearing things, what their paws measure, and which rooms you want to cover. A few quick checks will help.

**Step 1: Measure the paw width.** Stand your dog on a sheet of paper with their weight on the paw. Mark the widest points of the paw and measure the gap in inches. Do this on a front paw and a back paw, since they can differ. Compare these numbers with the maker's size chart (DOK measures the widest part of the paw while your dog bears weight).

**Step 2: Look at the nails.** If you are considering nail grips, check your dog's nail size against the maker's chart, and make sure your dog is comfortable having their paws handled.

**Step 3: Map the slippery spots.** Walk through your home and note where your dog walks most: the bedroom doorway, the hallway, the kitchen, or by the water bowl. Those are the zones to cover first.

**How to read it:** pick the row that describes your dog, then confirm the size on the product page. For the sock sizes, use the paw width you measured, and for ToeGrips, measure the nails on one front paw and one hind paw (the ToeGrips page uses floss marked and stretched flat, measured in millimeters) and size from the measurements rather than their average. The maker notes some dogs need a combination of two sizes. Many pet owners also keep a spray on hand alongside socks or nail grips so every room is covered.
