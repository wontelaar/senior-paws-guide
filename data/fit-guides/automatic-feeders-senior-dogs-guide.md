## Which Automatic Feeder Fits My Dog and My Routine?

Start with how you want meals to be handled, then check the feeder's hopper size and where it will sit.

**Step 1: Pick your control style.** Timed portions, app control from your phone, or no electricity at all each point to a different feeder below.

**Step 2: Check the hopper volume.** The listed capacities are in liters: 3 L is about 12.7 cups of volume, 5 L about 21.1 cups, and 7 L about 29.6 cups. Compare that with how many cups you would like to load at once.

**Step 3: Measure the feeding spot and your dog.** Measure the floor space in inches. If you are considering the elevated model, measure from the floor to your dog's lower chest while standing, and compare it with the stand height on the listing.
