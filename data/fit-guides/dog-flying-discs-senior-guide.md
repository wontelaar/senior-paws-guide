## What Size Disc Does Your Dog Need?

Disc size and material decide how easily your dog can grip, carry, and catch a throw. A quick check of your dog's weight and jaw width helps you match them to the right disc.

**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Use a home scale (step on holding your dog, then subtract your own weight) or check their last vet visit record.

**Step 2: Check your dog's mouth width.** Gently measure the width across your dog's open mouth, or simply note whether they're generally described as a small, medium, or large breed.

**Step 3: Match the disc diameter to their size.** Smaller dogs generally do better gripping smaller, lighter discs, while medium and large dogs can usually manage wider discs. If your dog has any jaw, dental, or joint sensitivity, check with your vet before starting disc play.

**Disc diameter at a glance**

| Disc diameter | Typically suits |
|---|---|
| 7 in (17.8 cm) | Small dogs |
| 9 in (22.9 cm) | Small to medium dogs |
| 10 in (25.4 cm) | Medium to large dogs |
| 9.75–10.25 in (24.8–26 cm) | Large dogs |
