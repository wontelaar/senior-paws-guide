## What Size Disc Does Your Dog Need?

Disc size and material decide how easily your dog can grip, carry, and catch a throw. A quick check of your dog's weight and jaw width helps you match them to the right disc.

**Step 1: Compare sizes.** The listed discs run from 7 inches to about 10 inches across, shown in the table below.

**Step 2: Compare materials.** The Hyper Pet is multilayer nylon, the Nerf and the KONG are rubber, and the Chuckit! Paraflight lists a floating disc built for long-distance flight.

**Step 3: Check the listed dog size.** The KONG lists small dogs, the Nerf lists medium and large dogs, and the Chuckit! is sized Large. The Hyper Pet does not list a dog size.

**How to read it:** the KONG Flyer is the smallest at 7 inches and is listed for small dogs. The Hyper Pet Flippy Flopper is a 9-inch multilayer nylon disc that floats, and its listing says it is not a chew toy, so supervise play. The Nerf Dog Atomic Flyer is a 10-inch rubber disc listed for medium and large dogs and as waterproof and weather-resistant. The Chuckit! Paraflight is sized Large and listed as a floating disc designed for long-distance flight. Confirm exact dimensions on the product page before ordering.
