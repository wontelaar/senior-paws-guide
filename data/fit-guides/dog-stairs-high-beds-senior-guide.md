## What Size Does Your Dog Need, and Does the Height Match Your Bed?

Dog stairs for high beds work best when the finished height lines up with your mattress and the steps suit your dog's weight. Here is a two-minute way to match your bed and dog to the stairs below.

**Step 1: Measure your bed height.** Measure straight up from the floor to the top of the mattress, where your dog will step off onto the bed.

**Step 2: Weigh your dog.** Stand on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight, so you can compare against each product's listed weight limit.

**Step 3: Check the step count and step depth.** More steps and deeper steps generally mean an easier climb for a senior dog; a vet visit is worth it if your dog has joint or mobility concerns before adding new stairs to their routine.

**Which stairs match which bed height?**

| Your bed height | A matching option |
|---|---|
| Around 24 to 24.5 in (61–62 cm) | YENING Large Dog Stairs (24 in) or Miorun Dog Stairs for High Beds (24.5 in, 5 steps) |
| Around 25 in (63.5 cm) | Miorun Extra Wide Dog Stairs (25 in) |
| 27 to 30 in (69–76 cm) | L-Shaped 6-Step Dog Stairs (27 to 30 in) |

**How to read it:** if your bed sits around 24 to 24.5 inches, both the YENING stairs and the 5-step Miorun line up closely, with the YENING's listed 100 lb capacity a helpful check if your dog is on the larger side. A bed near 25 inches pairs with the Miorun Extra Wide, which lists a wide 19.7 in base and an 80 lb limit alongside a plush, machine-washable cover. For taller bed frames between 27 and 30 inches, the L-Shaped 6-Step stairs are built for that exact range, with six steps to spread out the climb. All four options include a removable, machine-washable cover and a non-slip or anti-slip base for stability on the floor. Where a weight limit or footprint isn't listed above, confirm the exact dimensions on the product page before ordering.
