## Will It Fit Your Vehicle? A 2-Minute Ramp Length Check

Length is the first thing to check when you choose a car ramp: the taller your cargo floor, the longer the ramp you need for an easy slope. Here is how to match a ramp to your vehicle in two minutes.

**Step 1: Measure the rise.** Measure straight up from the ground to the spot your dog will step onto (the cargo floor, or the door sill for a back seat). Use inches.

**Step 2: Pick a comfortable slope.** Gentler is easier on older dogs. Guides for dog ramps commonly suggest staying somewhere around 18 to 25 degrees, and smaller or older dogs do best toward the gentle end.

**Step 3: Check the length.** The ramp length you need is the rise divided by the sine of the angle. We did the math for common heights:

| Rise (ground to floor) | Length for a 25° slope | Length for a 20° slope | Length for an 18° slope |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 in | 47 in | 58 in | 65 in |
| 24 in | 57 in | 70 in | 78 in |
| 28 in | 66 in | 82 in | 91 in |
| 32 in | 76 in | 94 in | 104 in |
| 36 in | 85 in | 105 in | 116 in |
