## Which Handheld Vacuum Fits Your Cleanup?

Handheld pet vacuums differ in attachments, brush type and charging, so a quick list of your surfaces points you to the right one.

**Step 1: List your surfaces.** Note whether you mostly clean upholstery, stairs, a mattress, carpet or a car interior.

**Step 2: Check for tight spots.** Seams, couch cushion gaps and stair edges are easier with a crevice tool.

**Step 3: Compare charging and weight.** Use the tables below to compare how each vacuum charges, its listed runtime and its weight where listed.
