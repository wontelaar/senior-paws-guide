## What Does Your Cleanup Actually Need to Cover?

The right handheld pet hair vacuum depends less on brand and more on where the hair ends up — couch cushions, a mattress, stair treads, or the car. Here is a two-minute way to match your space to the right tool.

**Step 1: List your surfaces.** Walk through the rooms (and vehicle) where your pet sheds most. Note whether it's mostly upholstery, bare stairs, a mattress, or carpet, since some tools are built with a specific attachment for each.

**Step 2: Check for tight spots.** Look for seams, crevices between couch cushions, stair edges, or corners of a car trunk. These need a narrow attachment rather than just a wide brush.

**Step 3: Think about charging habits.** If you tend to grab a vacuum on the fly without planning ahead, a quick-charging option matters more than one you have to remember to charge overnight.
