## Which Playpen Fits Your Dog and Your Room?

Two quick measurements, shoulder height and floor space, tell you which pen below suits your dog and your home.

**Step 1: Measure shoulder height.** With your dog standing on a flat floor, measure from the floor to the top of the shoulder blades (the withers).

**Step 2: Measure your floor space.** Measure the length and width of the spot where the pen will go, and note any furniture or doorways the panels need to clear.

**Step 3: Match the numbers.** Compare your dog's shoulder height with the panel height and listed dog height below.

| Measurement | Inches | Centimeters |
|---|---|---|
| Listed dog height, Amazon Basics pen (up to) | 16 in | 40.6 cm |
| Panel height on the Amazon Basics, FXW Rollick and Feandrea pens | 24 in | 61.0 cm |
| Feandrea square footprint | 55 x 55 in | 139.7 x 139.7 cm |
| Feandrea floor area | 3,025 sq in (21.0 sq ft) | 1.95 sq m |

**How to read it:** if your dog's shoulder height is 16 inches (40.6 cm) or less, the Amazon Basics pen lists that range directly. For other shoulder heights or a fabric pen, check each listing's height guidance, and confirm exact dimensions on the product page.
