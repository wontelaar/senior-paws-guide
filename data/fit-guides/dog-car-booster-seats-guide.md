## Which Booster Seat Fits Your Dog's Weight and Size?

Booster seats are matched by your dog's weight first and resting size second, so two quick measurements point you to the right one.

**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Stand on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight. Note it in pounds.

**Step 2: Measure the resting footprint.** Have your dog lie down the way they settle on a couch. Measure the length from the chest to the rump and the width across the widest point, in inches.

**Step 3: Compare with the listing.** Match the weight to the capacities below, then compare the footprint with the seat dimensions.

| Your dog's weight | Seats listed for that weight |
|---|---|
| Up to 20 lb (9.1 kg) | JOEJOY Deluxe Dog Booster Seat, EUHAMS Dog Car Seat, Lealchum Dog Booster Car Seat |
| 21 to 25 lb (9.5 to 11.3 kg) | EUHAMS Dog Car Seat, Lealchum Dog Booster Car Seat |
| 26 to 35 lb (11.8 to 15.9 kg) | Lealchum Dog Booster Car Seat |

| Seat | Listed weight | Listed size or height | Good match for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lealchum Dog Booster Car Seat | Up to 35 lb (15.9 kg) | 6-inch (15.2 cm) memory foam cushion | Larger small breeds; fits front or back seats |
| EUHAMS Dog Car Seat with 6-Inch Memory Foam | Up to 25 lb (11.3 kg) | 19.2 x 17.7 x 11.7 in (48.8 x 45.0 x 29.7 cm) | Dogs whose resting footprint fits that size |
| JOEJOY Deluxe Dog Booster Seat with Metal Frame | Up to 20 lb (9.1 kg) | Check the listing | Smaller dogs; adjustable buckle installation |
| Memory Foam Dog Booster Seat for Small/Medium Dogs | Check the listing | 6-inch (15.2 cm) elevation | Dogs who do well with top and bottom seat-fastening straps |

**How to read it:** find your dog's weight in the first table, then use the second to choose the features you like. For example, a 22 lb dog lands on the EUHAMS or the Lealchum, and a dog who curls up small has room in either. The Memory Foam Dog Booster Seat for Small/Medium Dogs does not list a weight limit here, so compare your dog's weight with its listing. If your dog is close to a limit, the next size up in capacity gives a little extra room.
