## Which Slow Feeder Bowl Size Fits My Dog's Meal?

The easiest way to choose is to match the bowl's listed capacity to the amount of food you already serve, with a little room left at the top so the maze channels have space to work.

**Step 1: Measure your dog's usual meal.** Scoop the amount you normally serve into a measuring cup and note the number of cups.

**Step 2: Add some headroom.** A simple way to do it is to divide your meal by 0.75, so the food fills about three-quarters of the bowl. That gives the capacity to look for.

**Step 3: Pick the bowl that covers it.** Use the table below, then choose the material and style you like.

| Your measured meal | Capacity to look for (meal ÷ 0.75) | Listed bowls that cover it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 cup (237 mL) | 1.33 cups | Outward Hound Fun Feeder Slo Bowl (Large/Regular) at 4 cups, MOPANG Ceramic Slow Feeder Bowl at 2 cups |
| 1.5 cups (355 mL) | 2 cups | Outward Hound Fun Feeder Slo Bowl (Large/Regular) at 4 cups, MOPANG Ceramic Slow Feeder Bowl at 2 cups |
| 2 cups (473 mL) | 2.67 cups | Outward Hound Fun Feeder Slo Bowl (Large/Regular) at 4 cups |
| 3 cups (710 mL) | 4 cups | Outward Hound Fun Feeder Slo Bowl (Large/Regular) at 4 cups |

**How to read it:** find your meal in the first table, then browse the bowls that cover it. The 2-cup MOPANG lines up with meals up to 1.5 cups, and the 4-cup Outward Hound covers meals up to 3 cups with room to spare. If you prefer stainless steel, the Feedoo is the match to look at, and if you like ceramic, the MOPANG is. Capacity for the Drop (Small/Medium) is not listed here, so check the listing before ordering.
