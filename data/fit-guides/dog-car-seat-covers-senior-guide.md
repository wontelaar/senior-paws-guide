## Which Seat Cover Fits My Back Seat and My Dog? A Quick Measuring Guide

A cover works best when it matches your seat and the way your dog likes to lie down. Three quick checks will point you to the right one.

**Step 1: Measure your back seat.** Measure the width of the bench from door to door along the seat cushion, then the depth from the seatback to the front edge of the cushion. Use inches.

**Step 2: Measure your dog's resting space.** Have your dog lie down in their usual position. Measure from nose to the base of the tail, and note whether they curl up or stretch out. A stretched-out dog likes more open surface than a dog who curls.

**Step 3: Note how your dog rides.** Some dogs like a hammock sling at the seatbacks, some like to see out through a window panel, and some settle best on a firm, flat base. That choice narrows the list quickly.
