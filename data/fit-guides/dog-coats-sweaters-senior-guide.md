## Which Coat Size Fits Your Dog? A Chest-Measurement Guide

Coat sizes are based on chest girth, so one measurement tells you most of what you need. Here is how to match it.

**Step 1: Measure chest girth.** With your dog standing, wrap a soft tape measure around the widest part of the ribcage, just behind the front legs. Keep it snug enough to stay in place but loose enough to slide a finger under.

**Step 2: Measure back length.** Measure from the base of the neck (where a collar sits) along the spine to the base of the tail.

**Step 3: Match the numbers.** Compare chest girth with the listed ranges below, and use back length wherever the size chart asks for it.

**How to read it:** write down your dog's chest girth first, then find the row that includes it. A wrap-style jacket like the Vecomfy or JoyDaog closes around the body, which is handy for a dog who is stiff getting dressed, while a pullover like the IECOii or KOOLTAIL slides on quickly for an easygoing dog. If your dog falls between sizes, compare the listing's size chart and return options.
