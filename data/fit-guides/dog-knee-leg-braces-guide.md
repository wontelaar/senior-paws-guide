## Which Leg Brace Matches the Joint and Side You Need?

Braces are matched to a specific joint, a specific side, and your dog's measurements. Three quick answers narrow the list.

**Step 1: Name the joint.** Your vet can confirm which joint to support: the knee on a back leg, the hock (the ankle-like joint lower on a back leg), or the wrist on a front leg.

**Step 2: Name the side.** Stand behind your dog, facing the same direction your dog faces. The right leg is on your right.

**Step 3: Measure and weigh.** Weigh your dog (hold your dog on a scale and subtract your own weight). With your dog standing, measure leg circumference at the points shown on the listing's size chart, using a soft tape.

|---|---|
| Knee (back leg) | Ortocanis Dog Knee Brace, MerryMilo Dog Knee Brace |
| Hock (back leg) | Labra Dog Rear Leg Hock Joint Compression Wrap |
| Wrist (front leg) | Walkin' Wrist Hugger Dog Brace for Front Legs |

**How to read it:** start with the joint, because each brace is built for one area. Then choose the side and size using your measurements. Sizes are tied to your dog's weight and leg dimensions, so compare your numbers with the size chart on the product page before ordering.
