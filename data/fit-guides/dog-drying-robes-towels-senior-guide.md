## Which Drying Robe Fits Your Dog?

The best robe is the one that sits comfortably and closes the way your dog prefers. Three measurements and one style choice get you there.

**Step 1: Measure back length.** With your dog standing, measure from the base of the neck (where a collar sits) along the spine to the base of the tail. This is the main number most robe size charts use.

**Step 2: Measure chest girth.** Wrap a soft tape measure around the widest part of the ribcage, just behind the front legs. Keep it snug enough to stay in place but loose enough to slide a finger under.

**Step 3: Measure the neck.** Wrap the tape around the neck where a regular collar sits, again leaving room for a finger. Write each number in inches and centimeters (1 in = 2.54 cm).
