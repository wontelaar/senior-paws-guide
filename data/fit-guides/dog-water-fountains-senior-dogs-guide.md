## Which Water Fountain Fits Your Dog and Your Home?

Think about three things: your dog's size, where the fountain will live, and how many dogs share it.

**Step 1: Measure your dog's shoulder height.** Stand your dog on level floor and measure from the floor to the top of the shoulder blades (the withers). Larger dogs are well matched to a deep, wide drinking area; smaller dogs suit compact models.

**Step 2: Check your outlet.** Look at the spot where your dog likes to rest and see whether a socket is nearby. If not, a battery or no-power model keeps the fountain exactly where you want it.

**Step 3: Count the dogs.** More dogs call for more water capacity.

**How to read it:** large dogs line up with the oneisall, and small or medium dogs with the PETLIBRO. The Heouvo and PetSafe suit households that want a manageable tank size or more than one dog drinking from it. The PetSafe is a plug-in model that holds 128 oz (1 gallon) and measures 14.3 x 14.3 x 9.3 in, so measure the spot near an outlet before ordering. The PETLIBRO capacity is not listed here, so check the listing for it to be sure it fits your space.
