## What Size Pool Does Your Dog Need?

A pool that fits means your dog can stand and turn around with room to spare. Here is a quick way to size it up.

**Step 1: Compare sizes.** The listed pool sizes run from 32 inches to 67 inches across, shown in the table below.

**Step 2: Check your space.** Measure the patio, yard or balcony spot where the pool will go.

**Step 3: Compare depth and drain.** Depths are 8 or 12 inches, and the Jasonwell and the 32 inch pool list a drain or water outlet.
