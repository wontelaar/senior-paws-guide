## Which Carrier Fits Your Small Dog's Weight?

Carriers are matched by your dog's weight and by how you like to carry, so the quickest route is a scale and a look at the stated limits.

**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Stand on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight. Note it in pounds.

**Step 2: Measure your dog's length.** With your dog standing, measure from the base of the neck along the back to the base of the tail, in inches. Compare it with the size information on the listing.

**Step 3: Match the weight limit.**
