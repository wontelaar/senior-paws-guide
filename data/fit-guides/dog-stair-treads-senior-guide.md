## How Many Stair Treads Do I Need, and Which Size Fits My Steps?

Matching tread size and pack count to your staircase takes about two minutes and a tape measure.

**Step 1: Count the steps your dog uses.** Count every step, or just the ones your dog uses most, such as the bottom few.

**Step 2: Measure one step.** Measure the width from side to side, and the depth from the front edge (the nosing) back to the riser. Use inches.

**Step 3: Compare with the tread size.** A tread should span the width of the step and cover the area where paws land.

**How to read it:** for a short flight or just the bottom few steps, a 4-piece set is a neat match. For a full staircase, a 15-pack covers up to 15 steps in one order. If your steps are narrower than the tread, the MBIGM set can be cut to fit with scissors. Press each tread down flat when you install it and check the edges every so often. If your dog seems unsteady on stairs, your vet is a good person to ask.
