## Which Carrier Fits Your Dog?

Soft-sided carriers are judged by one simple question: can your dog stand, turn, and lie down inside without being squeezed against the mesh? Here's a two-minute way to check.

**Step 1: Measure your dog.** With your dog standing naturally, measure from the base of the neck (shoulders) to the base of the tail for length, and from the floor to the top of the shoulders for height. Use inches.

**Step 2: Add a little room.** A comfortable soft carrier is usually a bit longer and taller than your dog's body, so they can shift position without pressing against the sides. Add roughly 2 to 4 inches to each measurement as a rough comfort margin.

**Step 3: Compare to the carrier's floor space.** Match your dog's length-plus-margin against the carrier's interior length, and check the stated weight limit matches your dog on a scale.

| Your dog's body length + margin | Fits a 17 in long carrier? | Fits a 20 in long carrier? |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 13 in | Yes | Yes |
| 14–17 in | Likely, check height/width too | Yes |
| 18–20 in | No | Likely, check height/width too |
| Over 20 in | No | No |
