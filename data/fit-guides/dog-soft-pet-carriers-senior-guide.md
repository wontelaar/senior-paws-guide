## Which Carrier Fits Your Dog?

Soft-sided carriers are judged by one simple question: can your dog stand, turn, and lie down inside without being squeezed against the mesh? Here's a two-minute way to check.

**Step 1: Check the listed size.** Compare the listed carrier sizes in the table below, from 17 x 11 x 11 inches to 20 x 12 x 12 inches.

**Step 2: Check the listed pet weight.** The Henkelion and Top tasta list up to 15 lbs, and the Top tasta advises choosing by your pet's length and height rather than weight.

**Step 3: Compare openings and carrying.** Look at the handles, shoulder strap, zippered openings and mesh windows each listing describes.

**Which carrier covers which dog?**

| Carrier | Listed size | Listed weight guidance | Listed for |
|---|---|---|---|

| Collapsible Soft-Sided Carrier with Mesh Windows | Check the listing | Check the listing | Cats and dogs |

**How to read it:** the Henkelion and Top tasta carriers share a 17 x 11 x 11 inch size and are listed for pets up to 15 lbs, and the Top tasta lists advice to size by your pet's length and height rather than weight. The Amazon Basics carrier is 20 x 12 x 12 inches with front and top zippered openings and mesh ventilation panels. The collapsible carrier lists a steel frame and a wooden support board with mesh windows on four sides, and its exact size isn't listed here. Confirm exact dimensions on the product page before ordering.
