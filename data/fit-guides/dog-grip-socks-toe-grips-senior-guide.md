## How Do I Measure for Grip Socks or Toe Grips?

Socks are sized by paw width, so one measurement taken while your dog stands is the key.

**Step 1: Get your dog standing.** Place your dog on a firm surface so the paw is naturally spread out, not relaxed or lying down.

**Step 2: Measure the widest point.** Hold a ruler or soft tape across the widest part of the paw, in inches. Measure a front paw and a back paw, since the two can differ.

**Step 3: Use the larger number.** Find it on the listing's size chart. If it falls between two sizes, compare both sizes on the chart and the listing's return options.

| Paw width | In centimeters |
|---|---|
| 1.5 in | 3.8 cm |
| 2 in | 5.1 cm |
| 2.5 in | 6.4 cm |
| 3 in | 7.6 cm |
| 3.5 in | 8.9 cm |

Use this when a size chart lists centimeters.
