## What Size Bed Does Your Dog Need?

Extra-large senior dogs need a bed long enough to lie flat and wide enough to stretch out without hanging off the edge. Here is how to match your dog to the right bed in about two minutes.

**Step 1: Measure your dog.** With your dog lying on their side in a relaxed, fully stretched position, measure from the tip of the nose to the base of the tail. Then measure their height from the floor to the top of the shoulder while standing.

**Step 2: Add a buffer.** A good bed length is your dog's nose-to-tail measurement plus 6 to 8 inches, so they can stretch out fully and shift positions without an edge of the bed in the way.

**Step 3: Check the bed's listed length against your dog's weight and length.** Match your dog's weight to the weight limits below, then confirm the bed's listed length covers your dog's stretched-out length plus buffer.

| Dog's stretched length | Recommended bed length (plus buffer) |
|---|---|
| 36 in | 42-44 in |
| 40 in | 46-48 in |
| 44 in | 50-52 in |
| 48 in | 54-56 in |
