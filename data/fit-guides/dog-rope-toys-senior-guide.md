## What Size Rope Toy Fits Your Dog?

Rope toys work best when their length and knot count match your dog's size and tug style. A quick measurement helps you pick the right one from this lineup.

**Step 1: Measure your dog's head width.** Hold a tape measure across the widest part of your dog's head, ear to ear. This gives you a rough sense of how much knot your dog can comfortably grip.

**Step 2: Note your dog's weight class.** Weigh your dog on a home scale (or step on with them and subtract your own weight), since several of these toys are listed by breed size rather than exact dimensions.

**Step 3: Think about play style.** A dog who mostly tugs with a partner does fine with a shorter rope, while a dog who likes tossing, fetching, or solo chewing benefits from more length to grab onto and more knots to gnaw on.
