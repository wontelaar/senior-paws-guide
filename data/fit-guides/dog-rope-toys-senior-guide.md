## What Size Rope Toy Fits Your Dog?

Rope toys work best when their length and knot count match your dog's size and tug style. A quick measurement helps you pick the right one from this lineup.

**Step 1: Check the listed dog size.** Three of these list a dog size: Feeko for large breeds, SHARLOVY for medium to large breeds and Fida for large and extra-large dogs. The Mammoth lists play uses instead.

**Step 2: Choose a pack.** Compare a single rope, a 2 pack and a 9 pack.

**Step 3: Think about play style.** Decide whether your dog mostly tugs, tosses and fetches, or chews, and compare the listed lengths and knots below.

**How to read it:** the Mammoth Flossy Chews Color Rope Tug is a single 15-inch rope listed for tossing, catching and tug of war. For large breeds, the Feeko 2 Pack gives you both a 27-inch and a 20-inch rope so you can match the moment, whether it is a long tug session or a quicker game. The SHARLOVY 9 Pack suits households with medium to large dogs who like variety, since it bundles a 27-inch rope with a round rope ball, a multi-knot rope, and a tug rope ball. If your dog is on the larger or extra-large end, the Fida Dog Rope Toy is listed for large and extra-large dogs and has the longest rope at 3 feet with 5 knots. Confirm exact dimensions on the product page before ordering.
