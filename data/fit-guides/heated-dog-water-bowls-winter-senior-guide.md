## Which Heated Bowl Capacity and Cold Rating Fits Your Setup?

Heated bowls differ in capacity, wattage and listed cold rating, so three quick checks point you to the right one.

**Step 1: Choose a capacity.** Compare each bowl's listed capacity with how much water you want available between refills.

**Step 2: Check your lowest outdoor temperature.** Look up the typical overnight low where the bowl will sit (a porch, patio, garage, or kennel run) and compare it with the listed ratings.

**Step 3: Check where it will sit.** Some bowls are listed for indoor or sheltered outdoor use, so match the bowl to the spot.
