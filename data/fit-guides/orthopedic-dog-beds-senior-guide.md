## What Size Orthopedic Bed Does Your Dog Need?

The right bed lets your dog stretch out fully. Here is a quick way to match your dog to the listed bed sizes below.

**Step 1: Measure the stretched-out length.** Wait for your dog to lie on their side, legs extended, and measure from nose to the tips of the back toes. Write it in inches.

**Step 2: Measure the curled-up length.** Measure from nose to the base of the tail while your dog stands. Dogs who curl up use less room than dogs who stretch, so this is your minimum.

**Step 3: Compare with the listed sizes.** Pick a bed whose listed size is at least as long as your stretched-out number.

**How to read it:** if your dog likes to lean on an edge, the Bedsure Original's bolstered sides are made for that. If your dog is larger or leaner, the SupportMax's extra-thick foam is aimed at senior and large dogs, and if your home runs warm, the CWAWZ's cooling gel is a natural pick. Listed sizes are the sizes in each product name or description; weight ratings are not listed in this guide, so check the product page for the weight range.
