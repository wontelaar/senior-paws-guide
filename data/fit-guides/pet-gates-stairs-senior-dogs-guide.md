## Which Gate Fits Your Doorway or Stairs? A Quick Measuring Guide

The right gate is the one whose width range covers your opening and whose height suits your dog. Two measurements get you there in a couple of minutes.

**Step 1: Measure the opening width.** Hold a tape measure level across the doorway, hallway or stair opening, wall to wall. Measure at the height where the gate will sit and again near the floor, since baseboards can add a little. Write down the smaller of the two numbers.

**Step 2: Measure your dog's standing height.** Have your dog stand on a flat floor and measure from the floor to the top of the shoulders (the withers). Compare it with the gate heights below.

**Step 3: Choose the mounting spot.** Pressure-mounted gates suit doorways and the bottom of stairs. For the top of a staircase, choose a gate that offers hardware mounting, like the Cumbor, and follow the manufacturer's installation instructions.
