## Which Food Container Fits My Dog's Food Bag and My Storage Spot?

Two things decide the right bin: the size of the bag you buy and the space where the container will live.

**Step 1: Read your bag's weight.** Find the weight printed on the front of the bag you usually buy, in pounds. Pick a container whose listed capacity is at least that number.

**Step 2: Measure your storage spot.** Measure the width, depth and height of the pantry shelf, closet corner or floor space, in inches, and include the lid in the height.

**Step 3: Choose the features you like.** Wheels, a collapsible body or a twist lid can all help it suit your routine.
