## Which Dog Stairs Match Your Bed Height?

Stairs work best when the top step lines up with your mattress. A two-minute measurement tells you which stairs below match your bed and your dog.

**Step 1: Measure your bed height.** Place a tape measure on the floor and measure straight up to the top of the mattress, where your dog will step onto it. Note it in inches.

**Step 2: Check your dog's size.** Weigh your dog (stand on a scale holding them, then subtract your own weight) and note their body width, since wider dogs appreciate wider steps.

**Step 3: See how tall each step is.** Divide the bed height by the number of steps. Here is the math for common bed heights:

| Bed height | Each step on a 3-step stair | Each step on a 5-step stair |
|---|---|---|
| 16 in (41 cm) | 5.3 in | 3.2 in |
| 20 in (51 cm) | 6.7 in | 4.0 in |
| 24 in (61 cm) | 8.0 in | 4.8 in |
| 28 in (71 cm) | 9.3 in | 5.6 in |
| 30 in (76 cm) | 10.0 in | 6.0 in |

These are averages for stairs that reach the top of the mattress. More steps mean a smaller rise per step.
