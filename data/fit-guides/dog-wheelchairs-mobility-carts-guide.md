## Which Wheelchair Style Matches Your Dog's Measurements?

Cart sizing starts with a few body measurements and your dog's weight. Your vet can confirm whether a wheelchair suits your dog and which style, so bring these numbers to that visit.

**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Stand on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight.

**Step 2: Measure hind-leg length.** With your dog standing or supported, measure from the point of the hip down to the floor.

**Step 3: Measure girth.** Wrap a soft tape around the widest part of the ribcage, then around the narrowest part of the waist, and note both.

**Step 4: Note front-leg coordination.** Rear-leg carts leave the front legs to do the steering; full-body frames support all four legs.

| Dog's weight | In kilograms | Style to look at |
|---|---|---|
| 6 to 33.5 lb | 2.7 to 15.2 kg | LetPetRun Full-Body Support Wheelchair for Small Dogs (listed range) |
| Larger dogs | Check the listing | Best Friend Mobility PRO Rear-Leg Dog Wheelchair (Large), made for larger dogs |
| Any size | Check each size chart | LetPetRun Adjustable and Nasjac rear-leg carts |

**What each cart covers:**

| Cart | Supports | Listed features |
|---|---|---|
| Best Friend Mobility PRO Rear-Leg Dog Wheelchair (Large) | Hind legs | Adjustable harness, all-terrain wheels |
| LetPetRun Adjustable Rear-Leg Dog Wheelchair | Hind legs | Adjustable |
| Nasjac Lightweight Portable Rear-Leg Dog Wheelchair | Hind legs | Lightweight, portable, reflective stripes |
| LetPetRun Full-Body Support Wheelchair for Small Dogs | Front and rear legs | Adjustable, 360-degree front swivel wheels |

**How to read it:** match weight first, then the support type. A dog from 6 to 33.5 lb (2.7 to 15.2 kg) who needs front and rear leg support lines up with the LetPetRun full-body frame, while the rear-leg carts pair with a dog whose front end is steady. Weight limits and leg-length ranges for the rear-leg carts are not listed here, so compare your numbers with each size chart.
