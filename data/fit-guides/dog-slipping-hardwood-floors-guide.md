## Which Fix Matches Where Your Dog Slips?

Match the fix to the spot, then check the size on the listing.

**Step 1: Note where the slipping happens.** A hallway or a path between two rooms points to a runner rug. Slipping all over the room points to socks, nail grips or a paw spray. Slipping on the stairs points to stair treads.

**Step 2: Measure a hallway in inches.** Measure the length and width of the strip you want to cover and compare it to the listed rug sizes below.

| Runner rug | Listed size | In centimeters | Other listed details |
|---|---|---|---|

| KOZYFLY Boho Washable Cotton Runner | 24 x 72 in, 0.2 in thick | 61 x 183 cm, 0.5 cm thick | Braided cotton, rubber backing |
| Washable Hallway Runner, 2x8 ft | 24 x 96 in | 61 x 244 cm | Low pile, non-skid backing |

**Step 3: Pick the grip for the paws or the stairs.**

| Option | Where it goes | Listed details |
|---|---|---|
| DOK TigerToes Non-Slip Dog Socks | On the paw | Thick grip, sizes small through large |
| Roilpet Non-Slip Dog Socks | On the paw | Double-sided grip, adjustable straps, machine washable |
| Dr. Buzby's ToeGrips | On the nails | Rubber grips, marketed for senior dogs |
| OUTTDOZ Paw Grip Traction Spray | On the paw pads | Fast-drying spray, no socks or boots |
| MBIGM Carpet Stair Treads | On each step | 15 treads, 8 x 30 in (20 x 76 cm) each |
| COSY HOMEER Non-Slip Stair Treads | On each step | 4 treads, 28 x 9 in (71 x 23 cm) each |

**How to read it:** if your dog crosses one long hallway all day, the 2x8 ft runner (96 in) covers the most distance and the two 2x6 ft runners (72 in) suit a shorter strip. If the slipping happens in several rooms, socks, nail grips or the spray go where your dog goes. For a full flight of stairs, the 15 pack covers more steps than the set of four. Confirm exact sizes on each listing before ordering.
