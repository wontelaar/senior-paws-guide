## Pad or Heated Bed? Which Setup Fits Your Dog

The easiest way to choose is to start from what your dog already sleeps on, then match the controls you want.

**Step 1: Check your dog's favorite spot.** If your dog already loves a bed, a pad that slides under or onto it keeps that familiar spot. If you want one product that does it all, an integrated heated bed is the simple route.

**Step 2: Measure how your dog lies.** Have your dog settle on their side and measure nose to back toes, plus the width of their body. The pad or bed should cover the area where your dog actually rests.

**Step 3: Pick your controls.** Some owners like a few simple levels, others like to fine-tune warmth and timing.
