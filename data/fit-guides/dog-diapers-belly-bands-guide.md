## Which Diaper or Belly Band Fits Your Dog?

Start with two quick questions: is your dog male or female, and do you prefer disposable or washable? Then take a few measurements so you can compare them with the size chart on the listing.

**Step 1: Measure the waist.** With your dog standing, wrap a soft tape measure around the belly just in front of the hind legs, where a belly band would sit. Keep it snug enough to stay in place but loose enough to slide a finger under.

**Step 2: Measure the hips (for diapers).** Wrap the tape around the widest part of the rear, over the hips. A full diaper wraps both the waist and the hips, so write down both numbers.

**Step 3: Note your dog's weight.** Many size charts pair the measurements with a weight range, so keep all three numbers handy.
