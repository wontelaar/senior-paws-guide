## What Size Dog and Walking Style Match Your Leash?

A 6 ft leash gives similar reach across styles, so the real choice comes down to your dog's size and what the handle and material are built for. Here's a two-minute way to match one to your dog.

**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Step on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight for an approximate figure in pounds.

**Step 2: Check your dog's pulling habits.** A dog that leans into the harness or lunges at squirrels puts more strain on your hand than a dog that walks loosely at your side, which is where a padded handle becomes worth noting.

**Step 3: Measure your hand or glove width if you want a precise width match.** Leash widths in this guide run from 3/4 inch (1.9 cm) up to 1 inch (2.54 cm); wider webbing spreads pulling force across more of your palm.
