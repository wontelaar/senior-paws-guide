## What Size Dog and Walking Style Match Your Leash?

A 6 ft leash gives similar reach across styles, so the real choice comes down to your dog's size and what the handle and material are built for. Here's a two-minute way to match one to your dog.

**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Step on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight for an approximate figure in pounds.

**Step 2: Compare handles.** Two of these list a padded handle, the Joytale and the Taglory, and the other two do not list one.

**Step 3: Compare widths.** Leash widths in this guide run from 3/4 inch (1.9 cm) up to 1 inch (2.54 cm), plus the Taglory's 1/2 inch (1.27 cm) rope.

**Which leash is listed for which dog?**

**How to read it:** if your dog falls in the 20 to 120 lb (9.1 to 54.4 kg) range and you'd like a padded foam grip plus the heft of climbing rope, the Taglory Rope Dog Leash lines up with that spec sheet. For a medium or large dog and extra visibility on low-light walks, the Joytale Reflective Dog Leash lists double-sided reflective stitching alongside its padded handle. Smaller or medium breeds, or anyone who wants a choice between a 1 inch and 3/4 inch width, match the listed range for the TAIDA Nylon Dog Training Leash. The PetSafe Nylon Dog Leash is listed in multiple lengths and widths beyond the 1 in x 6 ft version here, so confirm exact dimensions on the product page if you need a specific combination.
