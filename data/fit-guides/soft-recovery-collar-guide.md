## Which Soft Recovery Collar Fits Your Dog?

Soft collars are matched by your dog's size category and neck measurement, so a minute with a tape measure makes the choice easy.

**Step 1: Measure the neck.** Wrap a soft tape measure around the neck where a regular collar sits. Keep it snug enough to stay in place but loose enough to slide two fingers under, and write the number in inches and centimeters (1 in = 2.54 cm).

**Step 2: Note your dog's size category.** Decide whether your dog is small, medium or large. Each product below is listed for specific categories.

**Step 3: Compare with the listing's size chart.** Match your neck number to the chart on the product page for the exact range.
