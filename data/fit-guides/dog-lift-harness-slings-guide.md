## Rear Sling or Full-Body Harness? Find the Right Support for Your Dog

The best lift aid matches where your dog needs a hand. Here is a three-step way to choose before you order.

**Step 1: Note where your dog needs help.** Is it mostly standing up from the floor and managing steps (the back legs), or would your dog also like support under the chest, such as when climbing into a vehicle? Back-end help points to a sling, and whole-body help points to a full harness.

**Step 2: Measure your dog.** Weigh your dog (step on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight). Measure chest girth with a soft tape around the widest part of the ribcage, just behind the front legs. Measure body length from the base of the neck to the base of the tail. Keep these three numbers handy for the listing's size chart.

**Step 3: Think about your own lifting routine.** Handles are what you hold, so consider how many you want. Bending at the knees and keeping your dog close to your body makes any lift easier on you.
