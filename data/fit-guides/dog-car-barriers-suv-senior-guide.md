## Will It Fit Your Cargo Area?

A dog car barrier only works if its width matches the space between your SUV's side panels, right behind the second-row seats. Here's how to check that in two minutes.

**Step 1: Measure your cargo opening.** Open the trunk and measure the widest point between the two interior side panels, just behind the back seats, where the barrier will sit. Use inches.

**Step 2: Check the height you need.** Measure from the cargo floor up to where you want the top of the barrier to sit, and compare it to each listed height.

**Step 3: Compare to the barrier's adjustable range.** Each barrier below lists either a fixed panel width or a width it extends to. Match your cargo opening to the closest listed width or range.

| Listed width | Barrier |
|---|---|
| About 60 to 62 in (152 to 157 cm) | Gtongoko at 60.6 in, FEED GARDEN at 60.6 in total width, NOAMOO extending up to 61.9 in |
| Main panel, about 37 to 38 in (93 to 97 cm) | FEED GARDEN's 36.6 in main panel and CASIMR's 38 in main panel, each listed with two side panels |

**Which barrier covers which width?**

| Barrier | Listed width | Listed height | Extra details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gtongoko Dog Car Barrier | 60.6 in (153.9 cm) | 16.5 in (41.9 cm) | Attaches to headrests with 4 knobs, no drilling or tools |
| NOAMOO Dog Car Barrier | Extends up to 61.9 in (157.2 cm) | Check the listing | 6.19 lb (2.81 kg), listed for SUVs, vans, Jeeps, hatchbacks and trucks |
| FEED GARDEN Dog Car Barrier | 36.6 in (93.0 cm) main panel + two 16 x 12 in (40.6 x 30.5 cm) side panels, 60.6 in (153.9 cm) total | 16.5 in (41.9 cm) main panel | Two lock points on each side |
| CASIMR Dog Car Barrier | 38 in (96.5 cm) main panel + two side panels | 16.1 in (40.9 cm) main panel | 6.18 lb (2.80 kg), listed for the cargo area |

**How to read it:** if your cargo opening measures close to 60 to 62 inches edge to edge, the Gtongoko at 60.6 inches, the FEED GARDEN at 60.6 inches total and the NOAMOO extending up to 61.9 inches are the listed widths in that range. The FEED GARDEN and CASIMR combine a main panel with two side panels, and the CASIMR's main panel is listed at 38 inches. For headrest attachment, the Gtongoko lists a 4-knob, no-tool installation. As cargo openings vary by SUV model, confirm the exact width and height on the product page before ordering.
