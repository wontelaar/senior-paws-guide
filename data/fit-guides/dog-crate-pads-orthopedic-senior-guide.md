## What Size Crate Pad Fits My Dog's Crate?

A pad that lies flat from wall to wall makes the most comfortable bed. Two measurements are all you need.

**Step 1: Measure the crate floor.** Take out any old bedding and measure the inside floor, length and width, in inches. Measure inside the crate, not the outside frame.

**Step 2: Measure your dog lying down.** With your dog resting in their usual position, measure from nose to base of tail. The pad's length should comfortably exceed this number so your dog can stretch out.

**Step 3: Choose the closest listed size.** Pick the pad whose length and width are closest to your crate floor measurements, then confirm on the product page.
