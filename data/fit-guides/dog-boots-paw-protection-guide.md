## How Do You Pick the Right Boot Size? Measure the Paw in 3 Steps

Boots are sized by paw, not by your dog's weight, so a quick paw measurement is the most useful thing you can do before ordering.

**Step 1: Trace the paw.** Set a sheet of paper on a hard floor and have your dog stand with weight on one front paw. A paw bearing weight shows its true spread, so trace around it while your dog is standing.

**Step 2: Measure width and length.** Width is the widest point across the paw. Length is from the back of the pad to the tip of the longest toe, or to the nail if the size chart asks for that. Measure both front paws and use the larger numbers, since front paws are often a little bigger than the back ones.

**Step 3: Compare with the size chart.** Many charts list centimeters, so here are common widths side by side:

| Paw width (in) | Paw width (cm) |
|---|---|
| 2 in | 5.1 cm |
| 3 in | 7.6 cm |
| 4 in | 10.2 cm |
| 5 in | 12.7 cm |

**How to read it:** take your paw width and length to each listing's size chart and choose the size that covers both numbers. Then pick by closure: a split-seam opening or simple strap is handy when your dog prefers not to hold a paw up for long. Sizes differ by product, so confirm the chart on the page you order from.
