## What Size Bowl Does Your Dog Need?

Matching bowl size to your dog's meal portion keeps travel feeding simple and mess-free. Here's a two-minute way to work it out.

**Step 1: Measure your dog's meal.** Check how many ounces or cups your dog eats per sitting, using the measuring cup you already use at home.

**Step 2: Measure your pack or crate space.** Note how flat the folded bowl needs to be to slide into a backpack pocket, glove box or crate corner, and whether it needs to clip onto something like a leash or bag.

**Step 3: Match that to a bowl's folded footprint and capacity.** A bowl's folded height tells you how little space it needs when flat; its capacity (when listed) tells you if it covers a full meal or just water on the go.

| Folded height | What it suits |
|---|---|
| Around 2 in (5.1 cm) | Slides easily into a backpack pocket, car door pocket or glove box |
| Around 3.5 to 5 in (8.9 to 12.7 cm) unfolded width | Sits steady on a car floor, crate floor or picnic table |
| 34 oz capacity | Covers a full meal or a refill of water for one stop |
