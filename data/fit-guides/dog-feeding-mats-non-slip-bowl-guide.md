## What Size Feeding Mat Fits My Dog's Bowl?

A mat works best when the bowl sits comfortably inside the raised edge with a little room to spare. Three quick measurements are all you need.

**Step 1: Measure across your bowl.** Measure the widest point of the bowl across the rim, in inches. If you use a food bowl and a water bowl side by side, measure each one.

**Step 2: Add a margin.** A handy rule is 2 in (5 cm) of mat on every side of the bowl, plus 2 in between two bowls. One bowl needs a mat at least the bowl width plus 4 in each way. Two bowls side by side need a length of two bowl widths plus 6 in, and the width of one bowl plus 4 in.

**Step 3: Compare with the mat size.** Use the table, then check the mat's dimensions on its listing.
