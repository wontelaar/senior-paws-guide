## Will It Reach the Water Source You Actually Have?

The hose length and connection style determine where you can use the sprayer in your home. Here is a quick way to match your setup to the right attachment.

**Step 1: Measure the distance.** From your tub spout or sink faucet, measure out to where your dog will actually stand or sit for the bath (in a tub, a shower stall, or on a patio just outside a window).

**Step 2: Check your fixture.** Note whether you'll be hooking up at a tub spout, a sink faucet, or a showerhead, and roughly how wide that spout or faucet is.

**Step 3: Compare with the listed hose length.** A coiled or stretchable hose rarely reaches its full listed length once attached and stretched out, so give yourself some room.
