## What Size Bowl Does Your Dog Need?

Matching a bowl to your dog takes just a quick weigh-in and a look at how much food or water they eat per meal. Here is how to size it up in two minutes.

**Step 1: Compare capacity and size.** The listed capacities run from 20 oz to 8 cups. The table below shows each bowl's listed size in inches and centimeters.

**Step 2: Check the base.** The 5.5-inch set lists an anti-skid base and the 8-inch bowl lists a soft silicone ring on the bottom. Check the Pearhead and SPOT listings for base details.

**Step 3: Check the listed dog size.** The 5.5-inch set is listed for dogs up to 20 lbs and the Pearhead for small to medium dogs.
