## Which Snuffle Mat Size and Type Fits Your Dog's Meals?

Snuffle mats differ most in size, material and what food they hold, so two quick decisions point you to the right one.

**Step 1: Decide what you will serve.** Dry kibble and small snacks work in every mat below. If you serve wet food, look for the silicone mat, which is listed for both dry and wet food.

**Step 2: Pick the floor space.** Measure the spot where your dog eats and compare it with the listed sizes below.

**Step 3: Choose how you like to clean it.** Fabric mats are cleaned by hand or machine, and the silicone mat is rinsed. Check each listing for its washing instructions.

**Listed size by mat**

|---|---|---|---|
| AWOOF Pet Snuffle Mat | 18.8 x 18.8 | 47.8 x 47.8 cm | Felt cloth |
| Dogs Snuffle Mat (Polar Fleece) | 18.5 x 18.5 | 47.0 x 47.0 cm | Polar fleece |
| Small Silicone Snuffle Mat | 8.1 x 7.5 | 20.6 x 19.1 cm | Food-grade silicone |
| Bone-Shaped Snuffle Mat | 11.3 x 7.2 | 28.7 x 18.3 cm | Polar fleece |

**Which mat suits what you serve?**

| What you serve | Mats listed for it |
|---|---|
| Dry kibble or small snacks | All four |
| Wet food | Small Silicone Snuffle Mat (listed for dry and wet food) |
| A larger hiding area | AWOOF (18.8 in) and Dogs Snuffle Mat (18.5 in) |
| A compact spot | Small Silicone (8.1 in) and Bone-Shaped (11.3 in) |

**How to read it:** match what you serve to the second table, then match your floor space to the first. The two large fabric mats give the most room to hide food, the AWOOF folds into a bowl shape, and the silicone mat grips the floor with 84 suction cups. The bone-shaped mat has a built-in squeaker paw. Confirm exact dimensions on the product page before ordering.
