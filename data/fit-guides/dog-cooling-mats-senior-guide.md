## What Size Cooling Mat Does Your Dog Need?

A cooling mat works best when your dog can stretch out fully on it. A tape measure and a minute are all it takes.

**Step 1: Measure your dog lying down.** With your dog stretched out on their side, measure from the tip of the nose to the base of the tail. This is the length the mat needs to cover.

**Step 2: Measure the spot.** Measure the floor, crate or bed area where the mat will go, so the mat fits the space as well as the dog.

**Step 3: Leave a little spare room.** A mat a few inches longer than your dog gives room to shift and stretch. The table below uses 4 inches of spare length.

**How to read it:** find your dog's stretched-out length, then choose a mat whose row covers it. K&H lists its X-Large (38 x 27 in) for dogs over 45 pounds (about 20.4 kg), and the Rywell is the roomiest listed here at 44 x 32 in. Place the mat where your dog already likes to rest, along with shade and fresh water. If your dog seems overheated in hot weather, contact a vet right away.
