## What Size and Weight Does Your Dog Need?

Match your dog's body length and weight to the tub's basin size and weight capacity before you buy, so your dog has room to stand and turn during a wash.

**Step 1: Measure your dog's length.** Measure from the base of the neck to the base of the tail, with your dog standing naturally. Note it in inches.

**Step 2: Weigh your dog.** Use a bathroom scale (weigh yourself holding your dog, then subtract your own weight), and compare that number to each tub's listed weight capacity.

**Step 3: Check how your dog gets in.** Some tubs have foldable stairs so a dog can walk in; others are entered by lifting. Check the entry method on each listing.

**How to read it:** compare your dog's measured length with each listed basin length and your dog's weight with each listed capacity. The Furesh station lists the longest basin at 43.1 inches (109.5 cm) and comes with foldable stairs, so it is the one built for large dogs. The SereneLife lists support for pets up to 130 lb with adjustable height and a leash restraint. The Beberoad and VEVOR are described for small and medium dogs and cats; the VEVOR lists a tub weight capacity up to 220 lb, so confirm its basin dimensions on the product page before relying on that number for a larger dog.
