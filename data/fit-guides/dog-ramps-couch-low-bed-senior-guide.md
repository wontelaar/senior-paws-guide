## What Height Does Your Couch or Bed Need?

Matching the furniture height to a ramp's adjustable range is the fastest way to find a comfortable, low-angle climb for a senior dog. Here is a two-minute way to check.

**Step 1: Measure your furniture.** Place a tape measure on the floor next to the couch or bed and measure straight up to the surface your dog sleeps on. Note the height in inches.

**Step 2: Think about your dog's size and mobility.** A dog's size and comfort with steps versus a smooth ramp matters here; if your dog has joint or mobility concerns, check with your vet about which climbing style suits them best.

**Step 3: Match that number to each product's listed height range.** Some products offer fixed height settings, others a stated maximum furniture height, so line up your measurement with the ranges below.

**What does your measurement tell you?**

| Your furniture height | What to look for |
|---|---|
| 11–14 in (28–36 cm) | Fits the low end of a 4-height convertible ramp, or the BAQTALE's stated range |
| 14–18 in (36–46 cm) | Fits mid settings on the 2-in-1 convertible ramp, the BAQTALE's upper range, or the 16-inch ramp's listed coverage |
| 18–22 in (46–56 cm) | Fits the taller settings on the 2-in-1 convertible ramp (18.5 and 22 in) |

**Which product covers which height?**

| Product | Listed height coverage | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 2-in-1 Convertible Dog Ramp and Stairs (4 Heights) | 13.6, 16.1, 18.5, and 22 inches | Converts between ramp and stairs |
| BAQTALE Dog Ramp for Bed and Couch | 11.4 to 17.7 inches, 4 height settings | Ramp with locking tabs |
| Dog Ramp for Small and Medium Dogs, 16-Inch Height | Suits furniture up to 18 inches | Fixed ramp, 33-inch extended length |
| 17-Inch Folding Pet Stairs and Ottoman | Opens to about 34.8 x 17 inches as a ramp-stair | Folds to an 18 x 15.75 x 15.75 inch ottoman |

**How to read it:** if your couch or bed sits lower, around 11 to 17.7 inches, the BAQTALE's four height settings are built to match that range. For furniture up to about 18 inches, the 16-inch fixed ramp lists coverage up to that height, with a deeper landing on each step. If you want more flexibility across a taller range, the 2-in-1 convertible ramp offers four settings up to 22 inches and can switch between ramp and stairs. The 17-inch folding stairs open to a listed ramp-stair size and fold back into an ottoman, which is worth knowing if you want the piece to double as furniture between uses. Confirm exact dimensions on the product page before ordering.
