Watching an older dog's paws splay out on the kitchen tile is unsettling, and it's a common enough problem that a whole category of grip socks and toe pads has popped up to help with traction on hard floors. This FAQ walks through the common questions people have before buying, including sizing, fit, and how to choose between socks and toe pads.

### My senior dog keeps slipping on the hardwood floors — which grip option should I choose?

Grip socks and toe grip pads are designed to add traction between a dog's paw and a slick floor surface, which can help a dog feel more confident walking on tile, hardwood, or laminate. Because they work right at the paw, the added grip goes with your dog from room to room, making them a practical part of an older dog's everyday mobility routine. The options below range from strap-on socks to adhesive pads, so there's a style for most dogs. If the slipping is new, sudden, or getting worse, mention it to your veterinarian.

### Do grip socks work well alongside rugs and runners?

Yes — rugs, runners, and yoga-mat strips in high-traffic areas pair nicely with grip socks, and many households use both together. The difference is coverage: a runner only helps in the spot where it's laid down, while socks or paw pads travel with the dog into the kitchen, down the hallway, or wherever the slick flooring is. ### How do I know what size to order?

Sizing for these products is based on paw width, not overall dog weight or breed, and it's measured while the dog is standing so the paw is naturally spread out rather than relaxed and compact. Measuring while standing matters because a paw measured while a dog is lying down can come out smaller than it actually is in use, leading to a sock that's too snug. ### How snug should the socks be?

A secure, snug fit keeps the sock in place so the grip surface stays where your dog needs it, which is why getting the paw-width measurement right pays off. A quick look at the paw whenever you put the socks on or take them off keeps things comfortable, and it's fine to take them off periodically rather than leaving them on around the clock. Following the sizing chart and strap instructions on the listing gives you the fit the manufacturer intended.

Grip socks and toe pads are a practical way to help an older dog feel steadier on slippery floors, and with the right fit they give your senior the traction to move around the house with more confidence.
