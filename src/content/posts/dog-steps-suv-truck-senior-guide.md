For a dog who hesitates before jumping into the back seat or truck bed, getting in and out of the vehicle can become the hardest part of every car ride. Dog steps give an aging, small, or arthritic dog a defined, consistent way up and down instead of a leap. The right set depends mostly on your trunk or bed height, your dog's weight, and how much space you have to store it.

## Will It Fit Your Trunk or Tailgate?

The listed trunk-height range (or step count) is the fastest way to tell which dog stairs will reach your vehicle's cargo floor. Here is how to check in two minutes.

**Step 1: Measure the height.** Open your trunk, SUV hatch, or tailgate and measure straight up from the ground to the lip your dog needs to reach. Use inches.

**Step 2: Check your dog's weight.** Weigh your dog (hold them on a bathroom scale, then subtract your own weight) and compare it against each product's weight rating.

**Step 3: Think about storage.** Telescoping stairs collapse down small; folding stairs fold flat. Either way, check that the folded size fits where you plan to store it (trunk floor, behind a seat, or garage shelf).

## How to Choose

- **Trunk or bed height:** Measure the height from the ground to your trunk, tailgate, or back seat before buying. The Uross set <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C73QQV3Z?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">Uross Telescoping Dog Stairs for Car, 4 Steps (Fits 23 to 26 Inch Trunk Heights)</a> is designed specifically around a 23 to 26 inch range, so confirm your vehicle falls within it.
- **Dog's weight:** Check the load rating against your dog's actual weight, with some margin if your dog is still growing or you have multiple dogs who'll use the same steps.
- **Step count and rise:** More steps, like the 5-step Brulio <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GJ6WRP6Z?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">Brulio Foldable 5-Step Dog Stairs for Cars, Trucks and SUVs</a>, mean a shorter rise per step, while a ramp format like the Pawccoli <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HG1F2DQP?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">Pawccoli 76 x 17 Inch Foldable Dog Ramp and Stairs for Large Dogs</a> offers one continuous incline instead of individual steps.
- **Step or surface width:** A wider step, such as the 20-inch width on the PetRuna <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BWXHSJB9?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">PetRuna Extra Wide 20-Inch Dog Stairs for Large Dogs, 4 Steps</a>, gives a dog more room to position its paws, which can matter more for larger breeds.
- **Storage and portability:** Telescoping and folding designs collapse to different sizes, so think about where you'll store the set when it's not in use and whether you want to move it between vehicles.
- **Anchoring features:** Some sets include a tether or snap hook that attaches to the vehicle, which is worth considering if you want an extra point of attachment during use.
- **Surface material and cleanup:** Materials range from non-slip metal mesh to textured PVC that wipes clean, so factor in how easy the surface is to maintain if your dog tracks in mud or snow.

## FAQ

**How do I know which trunk height I need?**
Measure straight up from the ground to the edge of your trunk, tailgate, or back seat opening where your dog will step off. Compare that measurement to each product's listed height range or step dimensions, and check the listing if you're unsure whether your vehicle fits.

**Do dog stairs work for both cars and trucks?**

Always check your specific height and weight needs against the listing before buying.

**Should I ask my vet before using steps with my dog?**
