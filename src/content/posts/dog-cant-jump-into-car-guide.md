## How to Choose

- **Lower car or SUV**: the 62 inch PetSafe ramp is the shortest and folds for carrying.
- **Tall trunk**: a 71 to 76 inch ramp gives a gentler slope.
- **Tight space**: telescoping steps collapse to about 17 x 13 inches.

- **Needs hands-on help**: the sling for the back end, or the full-body harness for both ends.

## FAQ

**Is a ramp or are steps better for a car?**
A ramp is a continuous slope and steps are a staircase. Choose the one your dog is willing to walk on, and the one that fits your vehicle and storage space.

**How do I get my dog to use a ramp?**
Set it flat on the floor first and let your dog walk across it with encouragement, then raise it a little at a time.

**Can I use a lift harness with a ramp?**
Yes. A harness gives you a handle while your dog walks up a ramp.

**Will these fit my car?**
Check the listed length, width and height range against your vehicle before ordering.
