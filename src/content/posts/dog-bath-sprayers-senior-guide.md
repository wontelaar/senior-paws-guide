### What exactly is a dog bath sprayer attachment?

A dog bath sprayer attachment is a fitting that connects to an existing water source — a bathtub spout, a sink faucet, or a shower arm — and redirects the water through a flexible hose to a handheld nozzle. Instead of relying on a cup or a stationary tub faucet, you get a hose you can move freely around your dog's body, which makes it far easier to reach the belly, legs, and tail. Some versions are designed to slip directly onto a spout, while others divert water from a faucet or showerhead and may include extra features like multiple spray settings or a built-in brush.
