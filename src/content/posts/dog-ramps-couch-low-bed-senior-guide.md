When an older dog hesitates at the edge of the couch or stalls out before hopping onto a tall bed, it's rarely stubbornness — it's a sign that jumping has become harder on them. A ramp or step gives a gentler way up and down than jumping, and matching the height and surface to your furniture makes all the difference in whether your dog actually uses it. This guide compares four options built for couches, low beds, and senior dogs of different sizes.

## What Height Does Your Couch or Bed Need?

Matching the furniture height to a ramp's adjustable range is the fastest way to find a comfortable, low-angle climb for a senior dog. Here is a two-minute way to check.

**Step 1: Measure your furniture.** Place a tape measure on the floor next to the couch or bed and measure straight up to the surface your dog sleeps on. Note the height in inches.

**Step 2: Think about your dog's size and mobility.** A dog's size and comfort with steps versus a smooth ramp matters here; if your dog has joint or mobility concerns, check with your vet about which climbing style suits them best.

**Step 3: Match that number to each product's listed height range.** Some products offer fixed height settings, others a stated maximum furniture height, so line up your measurement with the ranges below.

**What does your measurement tell you?**

## How to Choose

- **Furniture height:** Measure your couch or bed before buying, since each product here is built around specific height ranges — matching this closely keeps the incline gentle and manageable.
- **Dog size and weight:** Consider your dog's size and, where listed, the stated weight capacity, so the ramp or stairs can comfortably support them.
- **Ramp vs. stairs vs. convertible:** Some dogs do better with a long, gradual ramp, while others prefer defined steps; a convertible option lets you try both.
- **Surface grip:** Look for non-slip pads, grip mats, or textured surfaces, especially if your dog has reduced mobility or confidence on inclines.
- **Foldability and storage:** If floor space is limited, a model that folds flat or converts into furniture (like an ottoman) is easier to live with day to day.
- **Washability:** Removable, machine-washable covers make it simpler to keep the surface clean, which matters for households with multiple pets or frequent use.
- **Styling and blending with decor:** If you'd rather not have visible pet equipment in your living room, an ottoman-style option can double as a functional piece of furniture.

## FAQ

**How do I know which height setting to choose?**
Measure from the floor to the top of the cushion or mattress surface your dog needs to reach, then pick the product and setting that matches that height as closely as possible — check the listing for the exact range each model covers.

**Will my senior dog need training to use a ramp?**
Many dogs take to a ramp quickly, especially with encouragement and treats near the base and top; going at your dog's pace helps build confidence.

**Are these ramps only for senior dogs?**
They're a helpful option for senior and arthritic dogs, but they also suit small breeds, recovering dogs, or any pet that could use a gentler way up and down than jumping; ask your vet if your dog has a specific health condition that affects mobility.
