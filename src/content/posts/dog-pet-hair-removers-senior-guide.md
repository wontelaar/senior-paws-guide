## How to Choose

- **Surfaces you need to cover** — think about whether you mainly need something for the couch, the bed, rugs, curtains, or the car, since some rollers are marketed across a wider range of surfaces than others.
- **Emptying mechanism** — a press-open compartment, a latch-secured bin with a button, or a brush design each offer a different way to clear out collected fur, so pick whichever feels most convenient for your routine.
- **Visual cues** — a translucent window that shows when the roller is full can make maintenance easier to stay on top of.
- **Reusability and refills** — all of these options are reusable without sticky sheets or refill cartridges, which can save on repeat purchases over time.
- **Portability** — if you want to use the same tool in the car as well as on furniture, look for one explicitly marketed for car seats.
- **Handle comfort** — a rubber or over-molded grip can make longer sessions more comfortable, especially if you're covering a large sectional or an entire bed.
