## Which Stand Height and Bowl Setup Fits Your Dog?

Raised stands differ most in their listed heights, so one measurement and a quick comparison point you to the right stand.

**Step 1: Measure your dog's shoulder height.** With your dog standing normally on a flat floor, measure straight up from the floor to the top of the shoulder blades, in inches.

**Step 2: Decide the height you want to try.** Adjustable stands let you start at one setting and change it later; fixed stands keep one height.

## How to Choose a Raised Dog Bowl Stand

- **Height range**: Compare the stand's listed heights with the height you want to try. Here they range from 5.5 inches to 16 inches.
- **Adjustability**: If you want flexibility, look for stands with multiple height settings rather than a fixed height.
- **Bowl width compatibility**: Check whether the stand is designed for a specific bowl size or can adjust to fit different bowl widths.
- **Number of bowls**: Decide if you need one bowl, two bowls for food and water, or a stand that includes both.
- **Material**: Stands come in wood, bamboo, and metal; pick based on the look you want and how it fits your space.
- **Mess control features**: If your dog tends to splash water or scatter kibble, a design with a spill-catching tray or an included mat can help keep the area tidy.
- **Cleaning needs**: Dishwasher-safe bowls or stands make upkeep easier if you prefer not to hand-wash after every meal.
- **Assembly**: Tool-free or hardware-included assembly can matter if you want to set up the stand quickly.
- **Household size**: For multi-dog homes, a stand listed for a range of sizes can serve more than one dog comfortably.
