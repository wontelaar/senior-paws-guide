## Which Crate Does Your Setup Need?

Crate covers are sized to the wire or metal crate they slip over, so the quickest path to the right cover is matching your crate's footprint and height to the cover's listed dimensions. Here's a two-minute way to check.

**Step 1: Measure your crate.** With a tape measure, record your crate's length, width, and height in inches — length and width along the floor, height from the floor to the top of the wire frame.

**Step 2: Note your crate's material and door layout.** Covers are listed for wire or metal crates and some leave specific openings for doors, so check whether your crate has a front door only or front-and-side doors.

**Step 3: Think about airflow.** If your dog's space can run warm, or you simply want to see in easily, a cover with adjustable openings, mesh, or roller shades gives you more control than a fully closed panel.

## How to Choose

- **Crate size and shape**: Match the cover dimensions to your crate's size — 36-inch covers like the Friends Forever <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C39Q1H5V?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">Friends Forever Metal Dog Crate Cover, 36 inch</a> and Amazon Basics <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DPQ4PFD6?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">Amazon Basics Dog Crate Cover for 36 inch Wire Crate</a> options are sized for 36-inch crates, while the Explore Land <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07KQVYWYX?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">Explore Land Dog Crate Cover, 42 inches, Black</a> and GORILLA GRIP <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C3WSDGPY?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">GORILLA GRIP Dog Crate Cover, 42 inch</a> covers fit 42-inch crates.
- **Door and access needs**: Think about how your dog's crate opens and how often you need to reach in — covers with multiple doors or zippered panels make it easier to access the crate without removing the whole cover.
- **Visibility and window options**: A mesh window with a flap, hook-and-loop window openings, or adjustable roller shades each give you a different way to control how much your dog can see out and how much light comes in.
- **Weight and handling**: Lighter covers are easier to slide on and off for daily use or frequent washing, while slightly heavier fabric can mean more material covering the frame.
- **Washability and upkeep**: If easy cleaning matters to you, look for a cover like the Friends Forever option that's listed as washable or one that's designed to slide off for cleaning, like the Amazon Basics cover.
- **Fabric and fastening style**: Oxford cloth, polyester, hook-and-loop fasteners, and zippers all affect how the cover fits and stays in place on the crate, so consider which fastening style suits your routine.
