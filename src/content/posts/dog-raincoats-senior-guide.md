## Which Raincoat Fits Your Dog's Shape and Walking Routine?

Raincoats are sized by body measurements and differ in how much of the dog they cover, so it helps to match both. It takes about two minutes.

**Step 1: Measure back length.** With your dog standing, measure along the spine from the base of the neck (where the collar sits) to the base of the tail.

**Step 2: Measure chest girth.** Wrap a soft tape around the widest part of the ribcage, just behind the front legs, snug enough to stay in place but loose enough to slide a finger under.

**Step 3: Measure neck girth.** Wrap the tape around the neck where a collar would sit, which matters for the hooded and collar-style coats.

Size charts often mix inches and centimeters, so here is a quick converter:

## How to Choose

- **Ease of putting it on**: Look for side-buckle closures, ponchos, or designs that go on quickly.
- **Coverage area**: Decide whether your dog needs back-only coverage, full four-leg coverage, or extra hip and thigh coverage, based on how they tend to get wet or muddy.
- **Leash compatibility**: If your dog already wears a harness, a coat with a leash portal or hidden leash hole means less undressing and redressing on walks.
- **Sizing**: Check size charts carefully, since a properly fitted coat will be more comfortable than one that's too snug around the shoulders or too loose to stay put.
- **Visibility features**: Reflective strips are a useful bonus for early morning or evening walks when light is low.
- **Weight of the fabric**: A lightweight shell is easy to carry and put on.
- **Adjustability**: Straps at the belly, chest, neck, or hood allow you to customize the fit.

## FAQ

**Why choose a raincoat?**
Many owners like having a waterproof layer for walks in wet weather, since it keeps cleanup after the walk simpler.

**How do I measure my dog for a raincoat?**
Check the specific sizing chart on each product listing, since sizing can vary between brands and styles like ponchos versus vest-style jackets.

**Can a raincoat be worn over a harness?**
Some designs, like those with a back leash portal, are made to work with a harness worn underneath, while others have their own built-in leash opening. Check each listing to see which setup fits your dog's existing gear.
