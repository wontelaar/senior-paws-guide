## How to Choose

- **Your dog's weight range:** Match your dog's weight to the leash's listed range — options above span anywhere from 25 lbs up to 150 lbs, so check the specific range for your dog.
- **Waist belt fit:** Look at the adjustable range of the waist belt (for example, 27–48 inches on one option) to make sure it will comfortably fit your waist size.
- **Number of handles:** Some leashes include a single waist handle, while others add a traffic handle or a second handle near the collar — useful if you want more than one way to hold your dog close in certain situations.
- **Storage needs:** If you like to carry poop bags, keys, or a phone, a leash with a zipper pouch or multiple pockets adds convenient storage during walks.
- **Visibility features:** Reflective stitching on the leash or belt can help with low-light walks, so consider this if you often walk early in the morning or after dark.
- **Bungee vs standard design:** Bungee-style leashes use shock absorbers, as listed on several of these products, which is worth considering if you want that kind of give built into the leash.
- **Material and construction:** Details like anti-tear nylon or padded neoprene handles can factor into your comfort and the leash's feel during use, so check the listing for specifics.
