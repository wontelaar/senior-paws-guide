Many senior dogs are happiest walking close to your side at an easy pace. A short traffic leash keeps that distance snug and predictable, giving you close control at crosswalks, in waiting rooms, or on crowded sidewalks. Below are the questions senior-dog owners ask most often when shopping for one.

### What exactly is a "short traffic leash" and how is it different from a regular leash?

A traffic leash is simply a short lead, often somewhere between 12 and 18 inches, designed to keep your dog close to your leg instead of several feet ahead or behind. For senior dogs, this short span is especially useful because it limits how far they can wander into a street, lunge toward a distraction, or stumble ahead on uneven pavement. Instead of gathering up a long leash, you get a purpose-built handle at the right distance, often with padding. ### What if my senior dog has started walking less steadily than before?

A short traffic leash is a great tool for keeping a dog close on everyday walks. Many owners pair one with a well-fitted harness, which spreads handling across the body rather than the neck. If your dog seems less steady than before, your vet can help. You can browse harness options alongside any of the leashes above to build a setup that fits your dog's current walking style.
