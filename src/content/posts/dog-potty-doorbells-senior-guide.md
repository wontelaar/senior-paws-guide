- **Range and volume control**: If you want to hear the alert from another part of the house, a wireless option with adjustable volume, like the TECKNET <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G39LB3NN?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">TECKNET Wireless Dog Door Bell with 55 Melodies</a> with its 1000 foot range and 5 volume levels, gives more flexibility than a hanging bell.
- **Waterproofing**: If the doorbell unit will be mounted near an entryway exposed to the elements, an IP65 waterproof rating is worth factoring into your decision.
- **Style and color options**: If you'd like the doorbell to match your home's look, options like the GINIDEAR <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09ZXFK4PF?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">GINIDEAR Handmade Dog Bells to Go Outside, Adjustable</a>, available in many colors, offer more personalization than a single-color design.
