## What Size and Reach Does Your Dog Need?

A retractable leash should match your dog's weight for safe handling and match your routine for how far you want them to roam. Here's a two-minute way to check.

**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Stand on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight, or use the number from a recent vet visit.

**Step 2: Think about where you walk.** Open parks and trails benefit from a longer tape or cord, while sidewalks and busy areas are easier to manage with a shorter length.

**Step 3: Match weight to a size category.** Retractable leashes are typically labeled by a maximum dog weight. Here's how common weight ranges line up with the length options in this guide:

| Your dog's weight | Suggested length range | Handle style to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 26 lb (11.8 kg) | 16 ft | Lightweight, soft-grip handle |
| Up to 55 lb (24.9 kg) | 16 ft | Anti-slip handle with thumb lock |
| Up to 110 lb (49.9 kg) | 26-30 ft | Rubberized or soft-grip handle with push-button or one-hand brake |

## How to Choose

- **Dog's weight**: Match your dog's weight to the leash's listed capacity — these range from small dogs up to 26 lbs to medium and large dogs up to 110 lbs.
- **Tape or cord length**: Shorter 16 ft options suit smaller spaces or dogs who stay close, while 26 ft and 30 ft lengths give more roaming room for dogs who like to range further.
- **Handle comfort**: Anti-slip, ergonomic, or soft grip handles make a difference on longer walks, especially if you're holding the leash for extended periods.
- **Locking mechanism**: Thumb locks, push-button locks, and one-hand brakes each offer a different way to control how much leash your dog gets — pick whichever motion feels most natural to use one-handed.
- **Weight of the unit itself**: Lighter leashes, like the 8.82 oz Fida model, may feel easier to carry for owners who prefer less bulk in hand.
- **Visibility features**: Reflective tape, like the kind on the Fida 16 ft small-dog leash, is worth considering for walks in low light.
- **Materials**: Options built with nylon, rubber, and stainless steel components add variety in how the leash is constructed — check the listing for full material details on any model you're considering.
