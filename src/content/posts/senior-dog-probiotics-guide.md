As dogs age, their digestive systems become more sensitive and less efficient at breaking down food and absorbing nutrients. For senior dogs—typically those over seven years old—this natural decline in digestive resilience often shows up as loose stools, gas, inconsistent appetite, or visible discomfort after meals. The problem compounds when arthritis or other age-related conditions limit a dog's mobility, because reduced activity can slow the entire digestive tract. What makes senior digestive issues particularly frustrating for owners is that the underlying cause isn't always obvious. A sensitive stomach in an older dog might stem from changes in gut bacteria, reduced enzyme production, food sensitivities that develop over time, or even the early stages of a condition requiring veterinary attention. That is where a well-chosen probiotic or digestive supplement can fit into a senior dog's daily routine.

Probiotics and digestive supplements are a popular way to give an older dog everyday microbiome support or enzyme supplementation. Understanding the main approaches available helps you pick the right match for your dog's needs and your own routine for giving it.

## Practical Steps Before You Buy

Before selecting a probiotic or digestive supplement for your senior dog, take a moment to assess your situation:

- **Start with a vet checkup if symptoms are new.** If digestive symptoms are new, worsening, or come with vomiting, lethargy, or weight loss, a quick vet visit is a sensible first step before supplementing.

- **Match the format to your dog's habits.** Does your dog easily swallow capsules or chews? Will it happily eat food with powder mixed in? Powder suits dogs who enjoy toppers, capsules can be opened or given whole, and soft chews suit dogs who treat them as snacks.

- **Compare the formulas.** FortiFlora lists a single strain, Proviable pairs multiple strains with prebiotics, and NaturVet adds digestive enzymes, so you can pick the approach that appeals to you.

- **Note the expiration date and storage instructions** when it arrives, and follow the label so the product stays as intended.

- **Follow the label for serving and duration.** Digestive supplements work as part of a steady daily routine, so consistency matters.

- **Track any changes in a simple log.** Note stool consistency, appetite, energy level, and any gas or discomfort before starting, then weekly as you continue. This gives you concrete data on how your dog is doing.

Selecting the right probiotic or digestive supplement for your senior dog comes down to your dog's habits and daily routine. A product your dog takes consistently fits easily into the day, whether it's a powder like FortiFlora or Native Pet, capsules like Proviable, or soft chews like NaturVet. Pick the format that suits you both, and you're well on your way to supporting your aging companion's digestion.
