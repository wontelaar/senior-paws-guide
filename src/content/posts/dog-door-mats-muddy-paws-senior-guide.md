## FAQ

**How often should I wash a dog door mat for muddy paws?**
This depends on how much mud and moisture your dog tracks in, but since all four mats here are machine washable, you can wash them as needed to keep them fresh — just follow the care instructions on the specific listing for cycle and temperature guidance.

**Will a mat like this keep my floors from getting wet?**
These mats are designed with absorbent chenille or microfiber surfaces listed to soak up significant amounts of moisture, which helps catch water and mud right at the door; check each listing for its specific absorbency details.
