## Which AirTag Holder Attaches to Your Collar?

AirTag holders differ in how they attach and what waterproof rating they list, so a quick look at your collar points you to the right one.

**Step 1: Check your collar.** Note its width and thickness, and whether it is a collar or a harness strap.

**Step 2: Choose an attachment style.** Slide-on silicone holders slip onto the collar band, the screw mount goes through the collar, and the loop case attaches with a removable metal loop.

**Step 3: Compare the listed ratings.** Use the tables below, and remember an AirTag is sold separately.

## FAQ

**Does the AirTag come included with these holders?**
No — all of these holders are designed to hold an AirTag, but the AirTag itself is sold separately in each case.

**Can these holders get wet or go outside in the rain?**
Each product lists its own waterproof rating as stated by the manufacturer, ranging from general waterproof/shockproof wording to IP68 and IP69 ratings, so check the specific listing for the exact rating that matches your needs.

**Will a holder fit any collar?**
It depends on the attachment style — slide-on silicone holders generally fit standard collar bands, while the through-collar screw mount is listed as fitting any collar width or harness up to a specified thickness, so check the listing to confirm it matches your dog's collar.
