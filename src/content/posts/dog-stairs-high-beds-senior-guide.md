## What Size Does Your Dog Need, and Does the Height Match Your Bed?

Dog stairs for high beds work best when the finished height lines up with your mattress and the steps suit your dog's weight. Here is a two-minute way to match your bed and dog to the stairs below.

**Step 1: Measure your bed height.** Measure straight up from the floor to the top of the mattress, where your dog will step off onto the bed.

**Step 2: Weigh your dog.** Stand on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight, so you can compare against each product's listed weight limit.

**Step 3: Check the step count and step depth.** More steps and deeper steps generally mean an easier climb for a senior dog; a vet visit is worth it if your dog has joint or mobility concerns before adding new stairs to their routine.

## How to Choose

- **Bed or sofa height**: Measure from the floor to the top of the mattress or cushion before ordering, since these sets are built for specific height ranges from 24 to 30 inches.
- **Your dog's weight**: Check the weight limit listed for each set — options here range up to 80 lb or 100 lb — and match it to your dog's size.
- **Step depth and width**: Dogs that like extra room to stand on each step may do better with deeper, wider platforms like the 20" x 11" steps on the YENING set.
- **Available floor space**: A straight staircase needs a longer run along the bed, while an L-shaped design can fit into a corner or tighter area.
- **Cover care**: All of these sets include removable, machine-washable covers, which makes it easier to keep them clean if your dog uses them daily.
- **Color and style options**: If matching your bedroom decor matters, look at sets like the 24.5-inch Miorun stairs, which come in six colors.
- **Unboxing and setup**: Foam stairs often ship vacuum-compressed or vacuum-sealed, so allow some time after opening for the piece to fully expand before use.

## FAQ

**How do I know what height of dog stairs I need?**
Measure from the floor to the top of your mattress or sofa cushion, then choose a set listed for that height range — the options here cover roughly 24 to 30 inches.

**Will the foam stairs fit my dog's size?**
Check the weight limit on the listing, since it varies by product, and consider the step width and depth if your dog is a larger breed that needs more room to stand comfortably on each level.

**Are the covers easy to clean?**
Yes, each of these sets has a removable cover that can be machine washed, which is useful for stairs that get daily use. If your dog has any mobility concerns, it's worth asking your vet for guidance on using steps or ramps around the home.
