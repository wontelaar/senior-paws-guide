## How to Choose

- **Size of the pad relative to your dog:** Compare the listed pad size with your dog and your space, from the SSRIVER's 51.2 x 31.5 inches to the more compact Ottomanson at 30 x 15 inches.
- **Tray included vs. sold separately:** The Ottomanson and LOOBANI sets come with trays, while the SSRIVER pad is listed as needing an additional tray for indoor use — factor that into your setup plans.
- **Drainage design:** If passing liquid through the turf matters to your setup, the PICK FOR LIFE pad's 63 drainage holes are worth considering.
- **Grass height:** The SSRIVER pad specifies a 1.18 inch grass height, which can help you compare texture expectations against other pads where height isn't listed.
- **Number of layers and spares:** The LOOBANI's 3-layer system with 2 replacement pee pads offers built-in backups, which can simplify rotation and cleaning schedules.
- **Material and care routine:** Check whether a pad is rinsed with soap and water or hand washed, and choose based on what fits your cleaning routine — materials across this list include polypropylene, polyethylene, and TPU.
- **Available space:** Measure your intended spot (near a door, in a laundry room, on a balcony) against each pad's listed dimensions before deciding.

## FAQ

**Do these grass pads need a tray?**
Some do and some don't. The Ottomanson and LOOBANI options come with a tray included, while the SSRIVER pad is listed as needing an additional tray for indoor use, so check the listing for each product before buying.

**How are these pads cleaned?**
Care varies by product: the SSRIVER and Ottomanson pads are rinsed with soap and water, while the PICK FOR LIFE pad is hand washed and rinsed. The LOOBANI set includes washable pee pads as part of its layered design.

**What size dog is each pad suited for?**
The Ottomanson pad is sized for small pets and dogs at 30 x 15 inches, while the SSRIVER (51.2 x 31.5 inches), PICK FOR LIFE (39.3 x 31.5 inches), and LOOBANI (35 x 23 inches) offer larger surface areas that can suit a range of dog sizes. Check each listing's dimensions against your dog's size before choosing.
