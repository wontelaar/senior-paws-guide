## How to Choose

- **Bed or sofa that needs protecting**: a waterproof blanket covers the most surface, and a washable pad covers one spot.
- **Few nights, low effort**: disposable wraps and a stack of pads keep laundry light.
- **Every night**: washable wraps, bands and pads can be washed and used again.
- **A male dog**: a belly band or a male wrap; for a female dog, a washable female diaper.
- **After an accident**: an enzyme cleaner for carpets, floors and fabrics, used as the label directs.

## FAQ

**How many pads or blankets should I have?**
Two of each makes washing easy, because one set is always dry and ready.

**Are washable wraps or disposable wraps better?**
Disposable wraps are convenient for travel and for nights when you want an easy change. Washable wraps can be reused. The two work well together.

**Can I put an enzyme cleaner on my dog's bed?**
The Rocco & Roxie listing names furniture, carpets, floors and clothing. Check the label and spot test fabrics first.

**What size wrap should I buy?**
Use the size chart on each listing. The Amazon Basics pack here is a large, the washable male wraps are sized by waist measurement (Medium is 15 to 17 in) and the washable female diapers are an extra large three-pack.
