## Which Fetch Ball Fits Your Dog?

Ball size should match your dog's mouth and jaw so fetch stays comfortable, and it needs to fit your launcher if you use one. Here is a two-minute way to check.

**Step 1: Measure your dog's weight.** Use a bathroom scale (weigh yourself, then weigh yourself holding your dog, and subtract) or check your vet records. This narrows down the ball size listed for your dog's build.

**Step 2: Check your launcher, if you have one.** Ball launchers are sized small, medium, or large. A ball needs to match that sizing to load and fire properly.

**Step 3: Compare the ball diameter.** Small dogs generally do well with smaller balls they can close their mouth around; medium and larger dogs can take a regular or medium-size ball. Here is how the listed diameters compare:

## How to Choose

- **Dog size and weight**: Match ball size to your dog's mouth and body — the Outward Hound set is listed for dogs under 15 lbs, while the Chuckit! and AMA SPORT balls are sized for medium dogs.
- **Launcher compatibility**: If you already own or plan to buy a ball launcher, check that the ball size matches it — both Chuckit! products are listed as compatible with Chuckit! launchers, and the AMA SPORT balls are listed as fitting regular launchers.
- **Material preference**: Rubber balls (like the Chuckit! options) and tennis-felt balls (like the Outward Hound and AMA SPORT sets) offer different textures and feel, so consider which your dog tends to prefer.
- **Pack size and backups**: Multi-packs like the Chuckit! set of 3 or the Outward Hound 8-pack mean you'll have replacements ready without an extra trip to the store.
- **Added features**: A squeaker, as in the Outward Hound balls, or a carry bag, as with the AMA SPORT set, can add convenience or extra engagement during play.
- **Floating ability**: If water play is part of your routine, the Chuckit! Ultra Rubber balls are listed as floating, which suits yard, pool, or lake fetch sessions.

## FAQ

**What size fetch ball is right for a small dog?**
Look at your dog's weight and jaw size first — the Outward Hound Squeaker Ballz are listed for dogs under 15 lbs, which makes them a fitting choice for small breeds, while the medium Chuckit! and AMA SPORT balls suit larger dogs who want a bigger ball to carry.

**Do these balls work with ball launchers?**
Yes — both Chuckit! products are listed as compatible with Chuckit! launchers, and the AMA SPORT tennis balls are listed as fitting regular ball launchers, so you can pair any of these with the right launcher for hands-free throwing.

**Is it better to choose rubber or tennis-felt balls?**
It comes down to your dog's preference and play style — rubber balls like the Chuckit! options offer a different feel and some float, while tennis-felt balls like the Outward Hound and AMA SPORT sets offer the classic tennis ball texture dogs often enjoy chasing and carrying.
