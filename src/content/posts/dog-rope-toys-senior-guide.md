## How to Choose

- **Dog size and listed breed guidance:** Match the toy to the listed dog size — the Feeko is listed for large breeds, the SHARLOVY for medium to large, and the Fida for large and extra-large dogs.
- **Rope length:** Longer ropes (27 inches to 3 feet) give more room for both dog and owner to grip during tug of war.
- **Number of knots:** More knots can give a dog more surface texture to grip onto, so consider whether a 4-knot, 5-knot, or multi-knot rope suits your dog's preferred chewing or tugging style.
- **Pack size and variety:** A single rope (like the Mammoth tug) is simple and portable, while multi-packs (Feeko's 2 pack or SHARLOVY's 9 pack) give you backups or a variety of shapes to rotate through.
- **Material and washability:** Natural cotton ropes that are listed as washable make it simple to keep toys clean between play sessions.
- **Weight:** The Mammoth rope is listed at 1.9 oz and the Fida rope at 750 grams, so the two differ a lot in heft.
- **Play style:** Think about whether your dog mainly enjoys tug of war, fetch, or chewing, and choose a set — like the SHARLOVY 9 pack — that includes multiple shapes if your dog enjoys more than one type of play.

## FAQ

**What are these rope toys made of?**
The Feeko and Fida ropes are listed as 100% natural cotton, the SHARLOVY set as natural cotton, and the Mammoth rope as a cotton-poly blend.

**How many rope toys should I keep on hand?**
Having a couple of options, like the Feeko 2 pack or the SHARLOVY 9 pack, means you can rotate toys to keep playtime interesting and always have a clean one ready while another is being washed.

**Can these rope toys be washed?**
The Feeko and Fida ropes are both listed as washable 100% natural cotton, so follow the listing's instructions for cleaning to keep them fresh between uses.
