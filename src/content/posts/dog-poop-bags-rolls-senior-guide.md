## How to Choose

- **Bag count**: Smaller counts like 120 or 150 suit light walking schedules or trying out a brand, while 540 or 1,000 counts fit multiple dogs or frequent walkers who prefer buying in bulk.
- **Dispenser and leash clip inclusion**: If you don't already own a dispenser, look for a set like the Amazon Basics or Gorilla Supply options that include one along with a leash clip or tie, so everything is ready to attach right away.
- **Bag size**: Both the Amazon Basics and Best Pet Supplies bags measure 13 x 9 inches, and the Gorilla Supply bags measure 9 x 13 inches — useful to note if you're matching bag size to your dog's typical waste volume.
- **Roll format**: Consider how the bags are packaged — refill rolls, perforated tear-off rolls, or multiple smaller rolls — since this affects how quickly you can access a fresh bag on a walk.
- **Material notes**: The Earth Rated bags are made with recycled material, which may matter if that's a priority in your purchase.
- **Certifications**: The Gorilla Supply bags are listed as ASTM D6954 certified, which some buyers look for specifically.
- **Scent**: All four options listed here are unscented, which is worth knowing if you have a preference either way.
