## How to Choose

- **Dog's size and weight**: Chews typically list dosing by weight and vests are sized by measurements, so follow the listing's guidance for both to get the right amount and the right fit.
- **Mobility and joint sensitivity**: If your senior is comfortable being handled and dressed, a snug vest adds physical reassurance; if your dog prefers less handling, a soft chew is an easy alternative.
- **Washability and daily wear**: A vest worn for storm season or regular vet visits earns its place in the routine, and a look at the care instructions keeps it fresh.
- **Budget and variety**: The budget-friendly Advanced Calming Chews make an approachable starting point, and a chew plus a vest can make a complementary pair.
- **Existing medications**: Since several chews contain active ingredients like melatonin, hemp, valerian, and chamomile, it's helpful to mention a new chew to your vet if your senior takes other medications.
- **Type of anxiety**: A dog anxious specifically during storms or fireworks may respond differently to a wrap than a dog with general separation anxiety, so think about when and why your dog's anxiety shows up.

## FAQ

**Why do some senior dogs become more anxious, and what can help?**
Aging bodies and brains change how dogs handle stress, and things like pain, hearing or vision changes, or cognitive shifts can add to it. A calming chew, a pressure wrap, or a combination of the two can add comfort to your senior's routine, particularly around storms, travel, and time alone.

**Should I try a chew or a vest first?**
It depends on the dog. Some respond better to the physical reassurance of a pressure wrap like the ThunderShirt <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0029PYC3K?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">ThunderShirt Calming Anxiety Vest</a>, while others do better with a supplement chew such as NaturVet Quiet Moments <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B008829WC4?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">NaturVet Quiet Moments Calming Senior Wellness Supplement (65ct)</a>. Many owners start with one option, see how their dog settles, and then add the other for a complementary routine.

**What's in a calming chew like the Advanced Calming Chews?**
Ingredients like melatonin, hemp, valerian, and chamomile are commonly included in calming chews for their calming properties, according to manufacturers. The Advanced Calming Chews combine all four in one soft chew, while NaturVet Quiet Moments is formulated with senior dogs in mind.
