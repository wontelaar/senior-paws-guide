## What Size Dog Fits Each Crate?

Soft-sided crates are sized by your dog's length and height, not just breed or weight, so a quick measurement check beats guessing. Here's how to match your dog to a crate in two minutes.

**Step 1: Measure your dog's length.** With your dog standing, measure from the tip of the nose to the base of the tail (where the tail meets the body). Use inches.

**Step 2: Measure your dog's height.** Measure from the floor to the top of the head or ears (whichever is taller) while your dog is standing naturally.

**Step 3: Compare listed sizes.** Compare your measurements with each crate's listed size and any listed dog weight or size, shown in the table below.

## How to Choose

- **Dog size and listed weight range**: Match your dog's size to the crate's listed dimensions and weight guidance — a 24-inch crate suits a much smaller dog than a 36-inch model.
- **Number and placement of doors**: A top door gives another way to reach in, while a front door suits dogs that walk in on their own.
- **Frame type**: Some crates use a lightweight pop-up frame, while others add a wire or steel frame for more shape — consider which style fits how often you'll be setting it up and folding it down.
- **Ventilation**: Mesh windows on multiple sides help with airflow and let your dog see out.
- **Portability features**: Carry handles, fold-flat storage, and tool-free assembly all matter if you're packing the crate in and out of a vehicle frequently.
- **Fabric**: Materials like polyester or water-resistant Oxford fabric affect how the crate holds up to everyday travel use — check the listing for cleaning guidance.
- **Multi-pet use**: If you have more than one type of small animal, a crate listed for dogs, cats, and rabbits can pull double duty.
