## Which Cooling Vest Fits Your Dog and Your Walking Gear?

Fit comes from three body measurements, and the choice between vests often comes down to whether your dog already wears a harness.

**Step 1: Measure chest girth.** With your dog standing, wrap a soft tape measure around the widest part of the ribcage, just behind the front legs. Keep it snug but loose enough to slide a finger under.

**Step 2: Measure back length.** Measure from the base of the neck, where a collar sits, along the spine to the base of the tail.

**Step 3: Measure the neck.** Wrap the tape around the neck where a collar sits. Compare all three numbers with each listing's size chart.

## How to Choose

- **Size and fit**: Look at the sizing chart on the listing closely and consider adjustable closures like zippers, hook-and-loop straps, or side-release buckles for a comfortable, custom fit.
- **Ease of putting on and taking off**: Quick-release buckles or simple wrap closures keep dressing quick.
- **Activation method**: Most cooling vests in this category work by soaking the fabric in water and wringing it out; some can also be chilled in a freezer beforehand, if you like a cooler starting temperature.
- **Compatibility with existing gear**: If your dog already wears a harness on walks, a vest designed to fit underneath it can simplify your routine.
- **Leash attachment**: Some vests include a built-in D-ring, which can eliminate the need for a separate harness during short outings.
- **Visibility features**: Reflective elements are a helpful bonus for early morning or evening walks.
- **Washability and upkeep**: Machine-washable options can make it easier to keep the vest fresh between uses.
- **Sun protection**: If your dog spends time in direct sunlight, an SPF-rated vest adds an extra layer of coverage.

As a general rule, never leave any dog in direct heat without access to shade and water.

## FAQ

**What do cooling vests do?**
Evaporative cooling vests are marketed to feel cool to the touch once wet, which owners like for warm-weather walks and time outdoors.

**How do I activate an evaporative cooling vest?**
Most of the vests in this roundup are soaked in cold water, wrung out, and then put on; some can also be dampened and placed in the freezer beforehand. Always follow the specific instructions on the listing for the product you choose.

**Can a cooling vest be worn with a regular harness or leash?**
It depends on the design. Check the listing to see which setup fits your current gear.
