## How to Choose

- **Handle length**: Compare the 26 inch Chuckit! Classic handles with the 12 inch Sport handle, which the listing recommends for kids and shorter hands.
- **Included balls**: The Chuckit! Classic with Ultra Balls comes with three balls, the other Chuckit! Classic with one medium ball, and the MEKAPLE with 15 mini tennis balls.
- **Dog size**: The 12M Sport is listed for medium dogs of 20 to 60 lbs. The MEKAPLE uses 2 inch balls, so compare that with your dog's mouth.
- **Hand or automatic**: A hand launcher needs no batteries, while the MEKAPLE lists three batteries included.
- **Weight and storage**: The 12M Sport weighs 5.6 oz, while the MEKAPLE weighs 3.8 lb.
