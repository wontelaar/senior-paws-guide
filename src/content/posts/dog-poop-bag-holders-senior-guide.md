## How to Choose

- **Attachment style** — look at whether the holder uses a click-and-lock strap, velcro, an elastic loop, or a carabiner clip, and match it to how easily you want to move it between leashes or bags.
- **Material** — options range from silicone straps and soft-touch plastic to ripstop nylon or polyester fabric, so consider what feels most comfortable in hand and suits your walking routine.
- **Bag capacity and included rolls** — most holders come with a starter roll of bags, so factor in how soon you'll need refills based on your walking frequency.
- **Used-bag storage** — some holders include a dedicated hook or separate carrier for a filled bag, which is useful if you prefer not to carry it loose until you reach a bin.
- **Extra attachment points** — a few holders add a D-ring or carabiner for keys, a clicker, or other small items, which can be handy if you like to consolidate your walking gear.
- **Slim profile vs. flexibility** — a slim holder stays unobtrusive on the leash, while an elastic-loop or clip-style design may give you more options for switching between a leash and a tote.

## FAQ

**Do these holders fit any leash?**
Attachment styles vary — some use a wraparound strap, others use velcro or an elastic loop — so check the listing for details on sizing and how it secures to confirm it fits your leash width.

**Can I use my own bags once the included roll runs out?**
Check each listing for refill compatibility, since dispensers are typically designed around a specific bag roll size or format.
