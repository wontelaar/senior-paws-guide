A pumpkin or fiber supplement can be a simple, low-effort way to support day-to-day regularity, but it's worth understanding what these products actually are (mostly fiber, sometimes with added enzymes) before picking one off a shelf. Below is a comparison of four widely used options to help narrow down what fits your dog's routine.

## How to Choose

- **Format preference**: Canned puree, powder, or chew — think about what your senior dog will actually eat without a fight, and what's easiest for you to store and portion.
- **Size and weight of your dog**: Chews like Glandex are dosed by weight, so follow the weight-based guidance on the listing to match the amount to your dog.
- **Ingredient simplicity**: If you want to avoid fillers or prefer organic sourcing, look closely at ingredient lists. Some products list just two or three ingredients, while others include a broader blend.
- **Specific concern vs. general regularity**: A general pumpkin or fiber topper suits everyday regularity support, while a formula with digestive enzymes, like Glandex, suits dogs whose routine includes scooting or anal gland concerns.
- **Storage and shelf life**: Canned products are ready to spoon, while powders and chews in resealable packaging are easy to keep on hand in the pantry.
- **Budget**: Prices and package sizes vary, so compare cost per serving to find the format that fits your budget.
- **Your dog's response**: Every dog's gut adjusts differently to added fiber, so introduce a new topper gradually and follow the serving guidance on the label.

## FAQ

**How do I choose a pumpkin or fiber supplement for my senior dog's regularity?**
These products are designed to support day-to-day digestive regularity, and each format suits a different routine: Fruitables is a canned puree for spooning over meals, Diggin' Your Dog Firm Up! and Native Pet are powders that mix into food, and Glandex is a soft chew. Pick the one your dog will happily eat. If a senior dog has new or persistent digestive symptoms, a vet visit is the right first step.

**How do I add a pumpkin supplement to my senior dog's regular food?**
Pumpkin and fiber toppers are designed to be mixed into meals. Start with a small amount, increase gradually, and follow the serving guidance on the label so your dog's gut can adjust comfortably to the added fiber.

**Which product suits a senior dog that scoots?**
