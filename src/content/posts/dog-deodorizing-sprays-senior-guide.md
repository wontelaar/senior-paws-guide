### What should I look for when picking a deodorizing spray for a senior dog?

Look for a spray described as pH balanced for dogs and paraben-free, with directions for regular use between baths. It's also nice when the formula does double duty by conditioning the coat. Sprays containing additions like oatmeal, aloe vera, or glycerin combine freshening with a bit of coat conditioning in one step.

### How do I actually apply these sprays to an older dog who may be less patient with grooming?

Every formula has its own application instructions, so follow the directions on the specific product's listing for how to spray and work the product through the coat. A calm, slow approach in short sessions keeps it easy, and it can pair nicely with a gentle brushing session.

### How often can I use these sprays?

These sprays are marketed for use between baths, which makes them convenient for keeping a senior dog smelling fresh day to day. Follow the usage guidance on each product's label, and if an odor ever lingers despite regular grooming, your vet can take a look.
