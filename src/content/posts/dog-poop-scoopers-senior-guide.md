**Step 1: Decide on a length.** Compare the listed lengths in the table below, from 28 inches up to 38 inches.

**Step 2: Pick a style.** Choose between a tray and rake set, a rake with a bin that feeds into a bag, or a spring scooper, and check the dog size a listing mentions, such as the TOOGE's medium and small dogs.

**Step 3: Think about storage and motion.** A tray and rake set has two pieces, while a foldable or one-handed spring scooper is a single tool you can use with one hand if the other is holding a leash.

## How to Choose

- **Handle length:** Longer handles, like the 38-inch tray reach on the TOOGE <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08YJ6MPSZ?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">TOOGE Pooper Scooper with Long Handle, Stainless Metal Tray and Rake</a> or the 32-inch reach on the Arm & Hammer <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B008FNKQSU?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">Arm & Hammer Swivel Bin and Rake Dog Pooper Scooper, Includes 2 Waste Bags</a>, let you clean up while standing fully upright.
- **Collection style:** Decide whether you'd rather use a tray-and-rake combo, a bag-fed swivel bin, or a spring-jaw scooper that scoops and holds waste directly.
- **Dog size:** Check the listing's guidance on dog size, such as the small-to-medium recommendation for the TOOGE <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08YJ6MPSZ?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">TOOGE Pooper Scooper with Long Handle, Stainless Metal Tray and Rake</a>, to match the tool to your yard's typical waste volume.
- **Portability and storage:** A foldable design like the SZHLUX <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C5XNGK5B?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">SZHLUX 28 Inch Foldable Pooper Scooper</a> is convenient if you're short on storage space or want to bring a scooper along when traveling.
- **One-handed use:** If you often walk with a leash or bag in one hand, an easy-pull, spring-loaded handle such as the one on the Heavy Duty ABS scooper <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07WPMXG7N?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">28 Inch Large Pooper Scooper for Dogs, Heavy Duty ABS</a> keeps the other hand free.
- **Material:** Stainless metal, like the TOOGE's tray and rake, and ABS plastic, used in the SZHLUX and Heavy Duty models, offer different builds worth matching to your preference.
- **Included accessories:** If you'd like bags on hand right away, look for a set that includes them, such as the Arm & Hammer's 2 included waste bags.
- **Weight:** All of these scoopers are under 2 lb, so comfort during extended use comes down more to handle length and grip style than overall weight.

## FAQ

**Do these scoopers work on grass, gravel, and other yard surfaces?**
Check each listing for details on surface recommendations, since tray, rake, and spring-jaw styles can behave differently depending on the ground. 

**Can I use a dog pooper scooper for more than one dog?**
Yes, these tools aren't limited to a single dog and can be used for routine cleanup wherever waste needs to be collected, keeping in mind the dog size guidance listed for a given model.

**Do I need to buy extra bags separately?**
The Arm & Hammer set <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B008FNKQSU?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">Arm & Hammer Swivel Bin and Rake Dog Pooper Scooper, Includes 2 Waste Bags</a> includes 2 waste bags to get started, while the other scoopers on this list collect waste into a tray or spring jaw rather than a bag, so check the listing if you want to pair one with bags of your own.
