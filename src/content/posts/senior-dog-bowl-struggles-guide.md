## How to Choose

- **Small dog**: the Vantic stand goes from 2 to 7 inches.
- **Large dog**: the PROERR stand lists 13 to 16 inches, and the COMESOON stand offers four heights with 50 oz bowls.
- **Messy eater**: the Neater Feeder Express contains spilled food; add a mat for the splashes.
- **Dog that pushes the bowl**: a weighted or rubber-bottom bowl.
- **Easy cleanup**: dishwasher-safe pieces and mats that roll or fold.

## FAQ

**Can I use a mat under a raised stand?**
Yes. A raised-edge mat can go under a stand and catches drips and kibble.

**Should I use a weighted bowl or a stand?**
They solve different things. A weighted bowl stays put on the floor, and a stand lifts the bowl. Some homes use a stand for food and a heavy bowl for water.

**Are the bowls dishwasher safe?**
The Neater Feeder Express lists its stand and bowls as top-rack dishwasher safe, and the two mats are listed as dishwasher safe. Check the listing for the other bowls.

**How do I know which height to pick?**
Compare the listed heights with the height your dog's bowl sits at now, and ask your vet if your dog has a health condition.
