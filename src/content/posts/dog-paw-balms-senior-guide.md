## How to Choose

- **Dog's size and weight**: Larger senior dogs may need product applied to more paw surface area, so look at how much balm is in the container relative to how often you plan to apply it.
- **Application format**: The Paw Soother is a stick applicator, while Bag Balm is a salve; pick the format that matches how your dog likes to have its paws handled.
- **Lick tendency**: If your dog tends to lick paws right after application, a formula marketed as lick-safe, such as PawSono, is a natural place to start.
- **Ingredient sensitivities**: If your dog has known allergies or sensitivities, compare ingredient lists closely. Palmer's highlights what it's made without (parabens, phthalates, gluten), which many owners appreciate.
- **Fragrance preferences**: Both Bag Balm and Palmer's are fragrance-free, a good match for dogs that prefer unscented products.
- **Budget and container size**: Container sizes vary (for example, the Paw Soother is 2 oz while Palmer's is 3.5 oz), so choose the size that fits how much you expect to use.
- **Easy cleanup**: Balms applied to paws can transfer to floors, furniture, and bedding right after application, so applying on an easy-to-wipe surface or a towel keeps things tidy.

## FAQ

**How do I choose a paw balm for my senior dog's cracked paws?**
Paw balms are designed to moisturize and protect paw pads from dryness and cracking, so start with the format you will enjoy using: a stick like the Paw Soother for direct pad application, a salve like Bag Balm, or a paw-and-nose balm like Palmer's or PawSono. If you notice bleeding, deep cracks, swelling, or limping, have a vet take a look.

**Which balm is easiest to use on a senior dog with arthritis?**
Senior dogs with stiff joints often prefer quick, gentle paw handling, so an easy-to-apply format helps. The Paw Soother's stick lets you apply directly to each pad, while the salves work well for owners who like to massage balm in. Compare the ingredient lists with your dog's sensitivities, and pick the formula and fragrance profile that fits.

**Which balm suits a dog that licks its paws?**
PawSono is the option here marketed as lick-safe, made with a food-grade, coconut-oil-based blend for both paws and nose. Whichever balm you choose, a few minutes of gentle distraction after application, such as a short cuddle or treat, lets it settle onto the pads.
