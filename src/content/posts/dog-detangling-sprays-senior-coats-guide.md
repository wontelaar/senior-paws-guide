- **Bottle size relative to coat length and grooming frequency.** An 8 fl oz bottle like BioSilk's may suit smaller dogs or occasional touch-ups, while a 16 oz size like TropiClean's or Vet's Best's may better suit larger dogs or more frequent brushing sessions.

- **Everyday brushing.** These products are made to soften tangles so a comb or slicker brush moves through more easily, which makes regular short brushing sessions smoother for a senior dog.
- **Comfort during application.** Arthritic or stiff dogs do best with short grooming sessions, so look for a product that applies quickly and works through the coat easily.

## FAQ

**How do detangling sprays fit into a senior dog's brushing routine?**
These products are designed to make brushing or combing easier. Apply the product, let it soften the coat, then work through gently in short sessions, which suits a senior dog who tires of long grooming. Regular, gentle brushing with a detangler on hand helps keep aging coats, which can mat more easily, smooth.

**Which detangler suits a senior dog with sensitive skin?**
Compare each product's scent and ingredients. Vet's Best is vet formulated with sage, aloe, cucumber, and vitamin B-5, BioSilk is pH balanced for dogs, and TropiClean uses a botanical blend. A small patch test the first time is an easy way to see how your dog likes a new product.

**How do I use a detangler on a stubborn knot?**
Spray or rub in the product, give it a moment to soften the knot, then ease through gently with a comb or slicker brush. TropiClean is marketed specifically to soften tangles, matted fur, and knots, and short sessions keep grooming comfortable for a dog with limited tolerance for long handling.
