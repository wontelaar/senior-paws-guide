**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Step on a scale holding your dog, then subtract your own weight for an approximate figure in pounds.

**Step 2: Compare handles.** Two of these list a padded handle, the Joytale and the Taglory, and the other two do not list one.

## How to Choose

- **Dog size and listed weight range:** Match the leash to your dog's size category — some of these are listed for small and medium breeds, others for medium and large dogs, and the Taglory rope leash specifies a 20 to 120 lb range.
- **Width:** Widths here range from 3/4 inch up to 1 inch and 1/2 inch rope, which affects how the leash feels in your hand and how substantial it looks on your dog.
- **Handle comfort:** A padded handle (like those on the Joytale and Taglory leashes) can make a difference in hand comfort on longer walks.
- **Material feel:** Nylon leashes tend to be light and flexible, while rope leashes like the Taglory offer a thicker, more textured grip — pick whichever feels more natural in your hand.
- **Hardware style:** Some leashes, like the TAIDA and PetSafe options, feature an easy on, easy off hook for quick clipping and unclipping.
- **Visibility needs:** If you often walk in low light, the Joytale's double-sided reflective stitching adds a visibility feature worth considering.
- **Weight of the leash itself:** Lighter leashes (2.4 oz to 4.8 oz) may feel less noticeable during walks, while a slightly heavier rope leash like the Taglory (9.59 oz) offers a more substantial feel.
- **Size options:** If you want flexibility to size up or down later, look for a line like PetSafe's that's sold in multiple lengths and widths.

## FAQ

**Is a 6 ft leash a good length for everyday walks?**
A 6 ft length is a common choice for daily walking, giving your dog room to move while keeping them within a manageable distance. The best length still depends on your dog's size, pace, and the environment you typically walk in, so check each listing for the exact length and width before choosing.

**What's the difference between nylon and rope leashes?**
Nylon leashes, like the TAIDA and PetSafe options, tend to be lighter and more flexible, while rope leashes, like the Taglory, offer a thicker, climbing-rope-style construction with a padded handle. Choosing between them often comes down to personal preference for grip and feel in the hand.

**Which of these leashes have a padded handle?**
The Joytale and Taglory leashes list a padded handle. Check the TAIDA and PetSafe listings for their handle details.
