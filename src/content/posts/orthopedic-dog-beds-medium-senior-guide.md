As dogs settle into their senior years, finding the right place to sleep matters more than ever — older joints and well-worn hips tend to appreciate a softer landing than a bare floor. A well-chosen orthopedic bed in the 28 to 30 inch range fits comfortably in most crates and living rooms while giving a medium-sized dog room to stretch out. This guide compares four highly-rated options, from flat foam mats to bolstered sofa-style beds, to help you find the right match for your dog's size, habits, and your household's cleaning routine.

## Will Your Dog Fit the Bed?

The right orthopedic bed should give your dog room to stretch out fully, with a little extra space to spare. Here's a quick way to check which bed matches your dog's size.

**Step 1: Measure your dog.** With your dog standing, measure from the tip of the nose to the base of the tail, then add the length of the front leg for a lying-down estimate. Measure shoulder-to-shoulder width too.

**Step 2: Add sleeping room.** Dogs tend to sleep more comfortably with a few extra inches of bed beyond their stretched-out length, so a bed should be a bit longer than a nose-to-tail measurement.

**Step 3: Compare to the bed's sleeping surface.** A sofa-style or bolstered bed has raised sides, so the usable flat sleeping area is smaller than the overall bed size. A flat bed uses close to its full listed size as sleeping surface.

| Dog nose-to-base-of-tail length | Suggested bed length to look for |
|---|---|
| 22 in (56 cm) | 26 in or more |
| 24 in (61 cm) | 28 in or more |
| 26 in (66 cm) | 30 in or more |
| 28 in (71 cm) | 32 in or more |

## How to Choose

- **Flat vs. bolstered design:** Dogs who stretch out fully tend to do well on flat mats like the EHEYCIGA Medium Bed or the OhGeni reversible bed, while dogs who like to lean or curl benefit from 3-sided or 4-sided bolsters like those on the EHEYCIGA Sofa-Style bed or the Spotted Play bed.
- **Thickness:** Options range from 3 inches up to 6.5 inches overall. Thicker, bolstered designs add loft and a raised edge, while lower-profile beds sit closer to the ground, which can matter for crate clearance or easy on-and-off access for a senior dog.
- **Foam type:** Egg-crate foam appears across all four picks, sometimes paired with memory foam for an added layer. If foam certification matters to you, the OhGeni bed notes CertiPUR-US certified foam.
- **Washability:** Every bed here has a removable, machine-washable cover, which makes a real difference for shedding, drooling, or muddy-paw households. Keep in mind that the foam inserts themselves are generally not machine washable, so spot-cleaning or air-drying the foam is the way to go.
- **Waterproofing:** Several beds include a waterproof liner or film layer under the main sleeping surface, which can help contain accidents or spills. Check each listing to see exactly which layer is waterproof, since in some cases the outer fabric or bolster sides are not.
- **Size fit for your dog:** All the beds in this roundup fall in the 28 to 30 inch length range, suited to medium-sized dogs. Measure your dog from nose to tail base and add a few inches so they can stretch out comfortably.
- **Non-skid bottoms:** If your floors are hardwood or tile, a non-skid bottom like the one on the EHEYCIGA Medium Bed can help keep the bed from sliding during use.

## FAQ

**How do I know if a 28 to 30 inch bed is the right size for my dog?**
Measure your dog's length from nose to the base of the tail while they're lying down, then add a few extra inches so they have room to stretch. Medium breeds generally fit well in this size range, but checking the specific listing dimensions against your dog's measurements is the most reliable way to confirm fit.

**Are these beds machine washable?**
The covers on all four beds are removable and machine washable, which makes regular cleaning manageable. The foam inserts themselves are not machine washable, so they're best spot-cleaned or aired out separately from the cover.

**Should I ask my vet about which bed is best for an arthritic senior dog?**
If your dog has an ongoing health condition or mobility issue, it's a good idea to ask your vet for guidance specific to their needs. Otherwise, choosing based on your dog's sleeping style, size, and your household's cleaning preferences is a solid way to narrow down the right bed.
