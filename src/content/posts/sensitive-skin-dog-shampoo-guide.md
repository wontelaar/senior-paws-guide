## How to Choose

- **Match the formula to the cause**: A quick check with your veterinarian on whether the skin issue is allergic, bacterial, fungal, or mixed points you to the right formula.
- **Consider your dog's size and coat type**: Thicker or double-coated breeds may use larger volumes per wash and benefit from thorough rinsing, so choose a bottle size that suits your dog's coat.
- **Evaluate washability and lather**: Look for a formula that rinses out easily; for arthritic or mobility-limited dogs, easier rinse-off reduces bath time stress.
- **Factor in frequency of use**: Antimicrobial shampoos (especially those with high chlorhexidine or ketoconazole) are typically used 2–3 times weekly for 2–4 weeks, not daily, so a smaller bottle may last longer than expected.
- **Match budget to strength**: Natural, milder formulas like TropiClean suit everyday care, Veterinary Formula is the budget-friendly clinical option, and Douxo S3 PYO and Nizoral offer higher-strength antimicrobial formulas.
- **Check for multi-species compatibility**: If you have both dogs and cats, Douxo products are formulated for both, whereas some alternatives are dog-specific.

## FAQ

**Q: How often should I use a medicated dog shampoo?**

A: Antimicrobial shampoos with agents like chlorhexidine or ketoconazole are typically used 2–3 times weekly for 2–4 weeks. After that phase, many owners transition to a gentler maintenance shampoo such as TropiClean Oatmeal & Tea Tree and bring the medicated formula back for flare-ups.

**Q: How will I know a medicated shampoo is working?**

A: Look for improvement within 2–3 weeks: reduced scratching, less visible redness or scaling, and improved coat appearance. Staying consistent with the product's directions gives the shampoo a fair chance to show results.

**Q: How do I choose between budget and premium medicated shampoos?**

A: Price reflects clinical backing, ingredient potency, and brand reputation. Veterinary Formula is a budget-friendly clinical option suited to mild hot spots and itching, while Douxo S3 PYO and Nizoral bring higher-strength antimicrobial ingredients for more involved skin concerns. Pick the formula that matches your dog's skin condition.
