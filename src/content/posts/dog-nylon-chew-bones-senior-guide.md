## What Size Chew Does Your Dog Need?

Matching a chew to your dog's weight and jaw size is the quickest way to narrow down these options. Here's a simple way to check in two minutes.

**Step 1: Weigh your dog.** Step on a scale holding your dog, then step on it alone, and subtract to get their weight in pounds.

**Step 2: Measure your dog's muzzle.** Measure the length of your dog's snout from the tip of the nose to just under the eyes, in inches. This gives you a sense of how long a chew they can comfortably hold and gnaw.

**Step 3: Compare to the package size.** Longer chews (measured by their longest listed dimension) generally give a dog more surface to grip, while compact chews suit dogs with shorter muzzles or smaller mouths. Use the table below to see how the listed length of each chew compares.

## How to Choose

- **Dog's size and weight** — check the listed weight range (like the Bam-Bones Plus T-Bone's 30-60 lb guidance or the Benebone 4-Pack's under-60-lb listing) against your dog's actual size before choosing a bone.
- **Shape and grip** — curved shapes like the Benebone Wishbone are designed to sit under a paw, while classic straight bones like the Nylabone Power Chew offer a more traditional hold.
- **Single chew vs. multi-pack** — a single Wishbone or T-Bone works well if you just want to try one shape, while the Nylabone 3-pack or Benebone 4-pack suit multi-dog households or anyone who wants backups ready to go.
- **Flavor preference** — options range from real bacon to beef, so consider what might appeal most to your dog if flavor tends to make a difference in how engaged they stay.
- **Material** — the Bam-Bones Plus line uses a bamboo fiber and nylon blend, which is worth noting if you're specifically looking to compare material types across chews.
- **Size dimensions for storage** — if you keep chews in a crate, toy bin, or travel bag, check the listed dimensions to make sure it fits your space.
- **Budget and value per chew** — weigh the cost of a single chew against a multi-pack to see which makes more sense for how often your dog goes through them.

## FAQ

**How do I know which size nylon chew bone to get for my dog?**
Check the weight range or size listed for each product — for example, the Bam-Bones Plus T-Bone is listed for dogs 30-60 lbs and the Benebone 4-Pack is listed for dogs under 60 lbs — and match it against your dog's current weight. If you're unsure which size is the better fit, check the listing details or ask your vet.

**Can I give a nylon chew bone to an older or arthritic dog?**
Many owners choose nylon chews for aging dogs because they don't require the same vigorous play as a fetch toy, but it's a good idea to check with your vet if your dog has a health condition before introducing a new chew.

**Is it better to buy a single chew or a multi-pack?**
It depends on your household — a single item like the Benebone Wishbone is great if you want to try one shape and flavor, while packs like the Nylabone 3-count or Benebone 4-pack are convenient if you have multiple dogs or want extras on hand.
