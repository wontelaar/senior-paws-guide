## How to Choose

- **Capacity vs. bag size:** Match the container's weight capacity (13 lbs, 25 lbs, or 40 lbs) to the size of the kibble bags typically bought, so refills fit without overflow.
- **Dog size and household needs:** A larger dog or multiple dogs typically go through food faster, which can make a higher-capacity bin like the 40 lb IRIS option more convenient, while a single small dog may be well served by a compact bin.
- **Available space:** Measure the spot where the container will live — a pantry corner, closet, or under a counter — and compare it to the bin's footprint, especially for compact or collapsible designs.
- **Mobility:** If the container needs to move between rooms or get wheeled out at feeding time, a bin with attachable wheels makes that simple.
- **Lid and closure style:** Decide between snap-lock latches and a twist-style Gamma Seal lid based on which closing motion feels easiest to manage, especially for anyone with limited hand strength.
- **Included accessories:** A built-in scoop keeps portioning consistent every day, and extras like a collapsible bowl can add convenience for travel or on-the-go feeding.
- **Portability needs:** For travel, car rides, or temporary storage, a collapsible design that folds down when not full can save space.
- **Material:** Look for BPA-free or food-grade materials, as listed for the IRIS bins and the Vittles Vault.
