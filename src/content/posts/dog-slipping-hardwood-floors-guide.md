## Which Fix Matches Where Your Dog Slips?

Match the fix to the spot, then check the size on the listing.

**Step 1: Note where the slipping happens.** A hallway or a path between two rooms points to a runner rug. Slipping all over the room points to socks, nail grips or a paw spray. Slipping on the stairs points to stair treads.

## Add Grip to the Paws

If your dog slips in several rooms, rugs cannot cover every path, and grip on the paw itself is easier. Socks cover the whole paw, nail grips attach to the nails and a spray adds traction to the pad, so the choice comes down to what your dog tolerates. Try one at a time and keep sessions short at first.

## Make the Stairs Safer Underfoot

Wooden stairs can be the slickest spot in a home. Peel-and-stick treads add a grippy surface to each step. Check that every tread stays flat and attached, that the edges do not curl, and replace a tread if it loosens. If your dog finds the stairs hard, a gate and a carry are a sensible plan as well.

## How to Choose

- **Hallway or one room**: a washable runner with a non-slip backing covers the most traffic with the least effort.
- **Several rooms**: socks or a paw spray go where your dog goes.
- **A dog who dislikes socks**: ToeGrips or the OUTTDOZ spray leave the paw uncovered.
- **Stairs**: treads give each step a grippy surface; the 15 pack suits a full flight and the four-piece set suits a short one.
- **Combining**: a rug in the hallway plus socks for open floors is a common pairing, and the two do not get in each other's way.

## FAQ

**Can I use a rug and socks together?**
Yes. They cover different parts of the home. A runner handles the hallway, and socks or a spray handle the open floor.

**How long should my dog wear socks?**
Start with a few minutes and build up, and take them off when your dog is resting. The Roilpet socks have adjustable straps, so you can change the fit as you go.

**Are these rugs machine washable?**
The listings describe all three runners as washable. The KOZYFLY listing says it can be vacuumed, shaken out or machine washed, so check each listing for the care steps.

**What if the rug moves on my floor?**
Each rug lists a non-slip backing. If a corner curls or the rug shifts, flatten it and check the backing before your dog walks across it.
