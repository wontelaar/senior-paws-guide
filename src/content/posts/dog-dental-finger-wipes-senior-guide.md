## FAQ

**Can dental wipes fully replace brushing for a senior dog?**
Wipes are marketed as a toothbrush alternative and are far easier for many older or handling-averse dogs to tolerate. Ask your vet whether a wipe-based routine is a good fit for your dog's specific needs.

**Do flavored wipes actually freshen breath or clean teeth?**
Flavor and freshening claims on these products are marketing language from the manufacturers, so treat them as a pleasant feature rather than a guarantee. For any concerns about plaque, tartar, or gum health, a vet dental check is the right next step.

**How do I pick a scent or flavor my dog will accept?**
Since older dogs can be particular, a multi-scent pack like MEDUWEDU <a href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CZN3PSTS?tag=seniorpawsgui-20" target="_blank" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener">MEDUWEDU Dental Care Finger Wipes (60 ct)</a> lets you try a few options without committing to just one flavor profile, and following the listing's instructions will help you introduce it smoothly.
