## How to Choose

- **Weight and handling:** A lighter vacuum is easier to maneuver one-handed around a bed or couch. The Shark is listed at 1.6 lb.
- **Battery and runtime:** Check how each model charges (USB, Type-C, or a dedicated charging dock) and consider how that fits your routine — a quick weekly tidy-up has different needs than cleaning up after every nap on the couch.
- **Brush type:** Motorized or roller brushes are built to work through pet hair embedded in fabric, while anti-tangle designs with rubber bristles are built for fur that wraps around brush rolls.
- **Attachments included:** Crevice tools and upholstery attachments make it easier to get into stair corners, along seat seams, and into car interiors, so think about where your dog spends the most time.
- **Dust bin and filter maintenance:** Easy-empty bins and washable filters cut down on the hassle of upkeep, which matters if you'll be emptying the vacuum frequently during heavy shedding seasons.
- **Where you'll use it most:** If you mostly clean stairs, a lightweight model is easier to carry up and down; if it's mainly the couch or bed, the brush and attachments matter more.
